## Current unresolved question: direct net worth or empirical lane scale

The owner's most recent concern was: STRATZ/OpenDota match payloads have native net worth, and each last hit yields different gold, so why convert a last-hit lane signal to gold? This is a valid challenge to the earlier phrase “gold per last hit.” The proposed numbers are **measured associations across earlier matches**, not a fixed payout formula. The owner has **not** approved them or answered the later V1 direction question. Do not treat the preceding discussion as a yes.

- The tracker already uses STRATZ `stats.networthPerMinute` from a match to measure the actual Mid and Offlane metrics ([historical query](../../../services/api/app/stratz/queries.py), [measurement](../../../services/api/app/tracker/metrics.py)). The same match's realized net worth cannot be an input to the pre-match opponent adjustment: that would let the result change its own comparison target. A direct-gold adjustment needs **prior, opponent-conditioned** net-worth evidence.
- The large retained STRATZ `heroStats.stats` aggregate gives net-worth means by hero and position, but no opposing-hero filter. The retained `heroStats.laneOutcome` aggregate is conditioned on opponent hero and exposes `csCount`, not net worth. Two owner-authorized, audited STRATZ schema reads confirmed these exact fields/arguments (both HTTP 200; operation names `TrackerMasteryGoldSchemaProbe` and `TrackerMasteryGoldFieldProbe`; zero OpenDota calls). Their private probe script is `.local/tracker-context-calibration-2026-09-27/scripts/tracker_mastery_gold_schema_probe.py`. Other STRATZ schema paths were not fully evaluated.
- The larger tracked-viewer history provides at most 8,633 Mid and 6,458 Offlane usable lane/draft examples for direct opponent-gold fitting. Median examples per opposing hero are only 28 Mid and 64 Offlane. Even a lenient 100-example requirement covers 71% of recent Mid drafts and 59% of Offlane drafts, below the existing 97% opponent coverage gate; at 500 examples, only one opposing hero per role qualifies. The 757 complete matches are smaller. These are upper bounds for direct gold because the count check did not require every viewer net-worth series to be complete.
- The provisional empirical lane scales are **48.63** for Mid lane net-worth advantage, **53.08** for Offlane lane net-worth advantage, and **45.31** for Offlane net worth at 10, measured in gold per unit of the last-hit-based matchup signal. Older/newer local sample estimates differ by at most 5.3%. They are held in the unapproved private draft.
- The last response recommended retaining the existing last-hit opponent model with its empirical gold scale for V1 because it has broad opponent coverage, while acknowledging that a direct-gold model would be a different data/methodology project. On resumption, explain this in plain English and ask **one direction question only**: keep that empirical V1 adjustment, or pursue a direct net-worth opponent model before public mastery. If the owner chooses direct gold, investigate other aggregate paths and/or a much larger independent historical match sample; update the foundation SSOT and versioned model before code changes.
