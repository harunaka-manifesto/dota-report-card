## Resolved: no lane-opponent adjustment

The owner asked why a last-hit lane signal should be converted to gold. Rather than build an empirical scale (48.63 / 53.08 / 45.31, now historical) or a direct net-worth opponent model (too little opponent-conditioned data: median 28 Mid / 64 Offlane examples per opposing hero), the owner removed the lane-opponent term entirely:

- No lane or opponent term enters any expectation, for any role or metric, including Carry Last Hits @10.
- `mid.lane_net_worth_advantage_at_10.v1` and `offlane.lane_net_worth_advantage_at_10.v1` keep the paired hero term (class B\*): viewer hero level minus the unique enemy counterpart's, both population values in gold. No conversion exists.
- `carry.last_hits_at_10.v1` and `offlane.net_worth_at_10.v1` are class B (own-hero term). Measurement of every metric is unchanged.
- The Difficult/Typical/Favourable badge remains, display-only. It never changes an expectation, state, PB or XP.
- Match Detail charts are unaffected; they read match timelines, not the expectation model.

Do not reintroduce a lane scale or direct-gold opponent model without a new owner decision.
