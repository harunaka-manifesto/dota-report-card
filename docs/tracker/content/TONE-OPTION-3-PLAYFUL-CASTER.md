# Tone option 3 — Playful caster

**Position:** a light match commentator. It draws on the small-step energy in [Duolingo](https://play.google.com/web/store/apps/details?hl=id&id=com.duolingo) and the informal, action-led framing in [GoPay](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=id&id=com.gojek.gopay). See the [research notes](LOCALIZATION-RESEARCH.md).

| Dimension | English | Indonesian |
|---|---|---|
| Address | you, your team | kamu, tim kamu |
| Rhythm | Punchy opener; fact follows | Pembuka singkat; fakta tetap lengkap |
| Celebration | Higher energy on verified wins and PBs | Lebih hangat untuk kemenangan dan rekor yang sah |
| Error/loss | Drop the playful voice | Kembali netral; jangan meledek |

Examples for the same facts:

| Use | EN | ID |
|---|---|---|
| Comeback | What a turnaround. Your team was down {deficit:,} gold at {minute}:00 and still won. | Berbalik menang! Tim kamu sempat tertinggal {deficit:,} gold pada {minute}:00. |
| Item timing | Early pickup: {hero} bought {item_name} at {time}. Median for this hero as Carry in Standard: {reference_median}. | Item cepat: {hero} membeli {item_name} pada {time}. Median hero ini sebagai Carry di Standard: {reference_median}. |
| Missing detail | We can't read this one yet. Try again later. | Detail pertandingan ini belum bisa dibaca. Coba lagi nanti. |

**Risks:** the opener adds length to small cards and can overstate borderline insights. It also needs a stricter review of humor on losses and data gaps. Use only if future player research shows the extra energy helps more than it distracts. Never invent causality or certainty.
