# Free DNA v6 copy review

This review records the deterministic copy guard for the v6 report payload.
Public text is assembled from finite family/outcome templates and the
server-owned claim contract; no LLM or browser-generated identity prose is in
the path.

## Guarded language

The scanner rejects causal or overclaiming terms including `because`, `causes`,
`aggressive`, `tilt`, `fatigue`, `positioning`, `fight entry`, `death quality`,
`personality`, `skill`, `MMR`, and inferred position labels. Identity,
interpretation, recommendations, share payloads, and nested recommendation
fields are scanned before publication.

Involvement is described as a context-adjusted rate, not aggression. Death
Exposure remains a descriptive exposure signal. Transfer, Post-Loss Response,
and Session Drift preserve mixed/unknown outcomes instead of inserting a
neutral guess. Recommendations state one supported metric, a locked baseline,
and a five-game verification rule; they do not claim improvement or causality.

## Review status

The checked-in deterministic scan and v6 fixture contract are the automated
review layer. Dota reviewer precision remains an external release gate and
must be recorded with the real calibration evaluation before the flag is
enabled.
