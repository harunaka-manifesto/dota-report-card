# Insight catalog

The registry in `services/api/app/insights/registry.py` contains all 22 MVP families:

- MVP-A summary families: adjusted role fit, hero-role residual, comfort versus stretch, hero-pool entropy, collapse tail, economy-to-impact efficiency, tower/objective orientation, item-timing reliability, duration curve, current-form divergence, recent style shift, and party/side/mode splits.
- MVP-B replay families: advantage conversion, deaths while ahead/high-net-worth deaths, early death tax, objective follow-through, power-spike conversion, farm-to-fight pivot, lane-loss recovery, comeback/trailing-side safety, teamfight survival conversion, and objective-vision timing.

Next-Rank Gap, archetype clustering, post-won-fight overreach, automatic replay parsing, and LLM-assisted tone remain deferred.
