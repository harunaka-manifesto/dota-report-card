# ADR 0001: Free DNA V6.1 additive generation

Date: 2026-08-24  
Status: accepted for implementation; not approved for public release

## Context

Free DNA V5.2 and V6.0 are persisted, versioned report contracts. The V6.1
research program needs richer summary-only estimates, a large private signal
surface, hierarchical semantic outcomes, and interaction-aware evidence without
relabeling historical snapshots or widening the Free data boundary.

## Decision

1. V6.1 is a distinct `free-dna-report-6.1.0` generation selected by its own
   disabled-by-default flag and strict validator. V5.2 and V6.0 remain
   immutable and readable under their original registries and copy.
2. The public ontology remains seven Elements and five top-level finding
   families. Richer research features live in a typed 128-signal private graph;
   only selected evidence can cross the public serializer.
3. Semantic outcomes form a finite checked-in registry below the five roots.
   Family-level correction precedes within-family branch correction.
4. Lifecycle, era, and behavioral-loop candidates remain shadow-only behind
   separate experimental flags. They have no public serialization or share
   entitlement.
5. Free uses one physical 365-day summary-history request with a canonical
   projection and audit hashes. Detail, parse, rank, and MMR inputs remain
   forbidden.
6. State A fixture/synthetic completion, State B real-corpus calibration, and
   State C release readiness are separate. Public rollout is fail-closed until
   State C and explicit operator authorization.

## Consequences

V6.1 can evolve without compatibility aliases or silent V6.0 behavior changes.
The cost is a separate artifact family, schema, model fingerprint, release
evidence set, and documentation surface. Private signal breadth does not imply
public claim breadth. Experimental candidates may be measured safely but cannot
appear in public responses, share cards, analytics dimensions, or Deep cohort
membership.
