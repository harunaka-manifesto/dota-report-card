## Strategy

During the laning stage, prioritize stealing damage of the enemy core and win the last hit battle off of it.

You can switch your target and hit something else during Static Link.

You want to hit opponents or creeps with the Plasma Field from maximum range for maximum value.

The Plasma Field provides vision so you can use it to scout tree lines, pillars and Roshan pit.

Use Eye of the Storm to speed up your farming, especially when clearing stacks and ancients.

Mobility and items that allow you to be in the middle of the fight, such as a {blink-dagger}, will enable you to get a great Static Link off.

Most of your fight impact will come down to Static Link usage. Take your time and use it on the proper target, preferably an immobile enemy core who predominantly does right click damage.

When not much is happening, push out the side lanes with Plasma Field without even showing yourself.

When you have your {black-king-bar}, target the enemy carry in fights and run at them with all your spells.

Pick up the {aghanims-shard} once you have {black-king-bar} to make use of your survivability to do more damage and slow down enemy heroes with Storm Surge during Eye of the Storm.

## Counter Strategy

Razor wins most lanes against melee heroes. Consider sending ranged heroes against him.

Getting movement speed items helps a lot at dealing with Razor's Static Link.

Keep a good distance to Razor and make it hard for him to get a good Static Link off.

Prevent Razor from draining your core's damage. That way, he will have less impact in fights.

{force-staff}, {hurricane-pike}, {linkens-sphere} and {lotus-orb} are great at dealing with Static Link.
