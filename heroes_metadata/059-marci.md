## Strategy

Look to Dispose an enemy in direction of your allies or to slow more enemies on landing.

You can save an ally by using Rebound on him as it provides movement speed bonus.

Many spells and items can kite Marci during Unleash. Counter them with items or choose the right moment to engage and be ready to switch targets.

{blink-dagger} is a huge power spike as it allows you to lock on or switch targets, initiate fights or save allies.

Unleash and Bodyguard are great tools for killing Roshan.

Look to play with one of your cores with Bodyguard to get kills on the map.

Use Bodyguard on your laning partner to have a massive damage and sustain advantage when playing aggressively.

With the alternate cast on Rebound, you can use the spell to reposition an ally along with your hero to bring them in or out of fights.

## Counter Strategy

Someone should buy {spirit-vessel} against Marci's Bodyguard.

Avoid staying too close to Marci as you'll get Disposed easily.

Kite Marci's ultimate by either stopping her from attacking or disengaging.

Acquire items that allow you to kite Marci's ultimate defensively or offensively, such as {heavens-halberd}, {ghost-scepter}, or {force-staff}.

Items that reduce healing and regeneration are good against Marci's Bodyguard.

Items that can dispel the Rebound damage barrier from Marci or her ally are great purchase.

Be aware of Marci's {blink-dagger} timing. She can easily gap close, setup kills with Dispose or lock on targets during Unleash.
