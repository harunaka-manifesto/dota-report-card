## Strategy

Quas Exort: Sunstrike opponents fountain to check opponents items and their lanes.

Quas Exort: Look around the map as much as you can for potential Sunstrike kills.

Quas Exort: You can use Sunstrike here and there to secure ranged creep last hit.

Quas Wex: Use Tornado and EMP to secure a last hit or deny a creep, and burn opponents mana at the same time.

Quas Exort: Use Forged Spirits to control runes.

Quas Wex: Control active runes and look for opportunities to rotate once you have {boots-of-speed} and {spirit-vessel}.

Quas Exort: Avoid rotating and running around the map. Instead, look to farm, pressure the enemy mid lane tower, and look for Sun Strike kills from afar.

Quas Wex: After ganking few times with {meteor-hammer} or {spirit-vessel}, consider going back for {hand-of-midas} if the game slows down.

When not much is happening, you can easily split push with Forge Spirits, or by dumping spells like Meteor, Tornado, and Deafening Blast from fog.

Don't forget to use Alacrity on yourself or a right clicking ally in fights. Works well with Cold Snap.

Use Forged Spirits or Sunstrike to scout if opponents are missing.

Invoker is great at enabling team to take Roshan early due to Alacrity, Forged Spirits and Cold Snap.

{aghanims-scepter} is a huge power spike, especially with Cataclysm, if you have a setup within the team, such as Chronosphere.

## Counter Strategy

Identify which Invoker type you are playing against, by looking at invoked spells and orbs above Invoker. Quas Exort Invoker is farm and burst oriented and uses the Sunstrike ability early on. While Quas Wex is ganking and control oriented and uses Tornado + EMP and Cold Snap + {urn-of-shadows} or {meteor-hammer} combinations.

Invoker is squishy and the only real defense he has are his spells. Try to gank him early in the game with {smoke-of-deceit} and {dust-of-appearance}.

Opt for anti-magic damage items against Invoker

Dodging or surviving Invoker's opening combination of spells, makes him weak for 5 to 10 seconds depending on the cooldown of his Invoke ability.

Keep in mind that Invoker's Tornado dispels. Active rune buffs and many spells are countered by it.

Invoker tends to pick up Cataclysm once he has {aghanims-scepter}. Be aware of it and be mindful about your hero's position when the Sun Strikes come in.
