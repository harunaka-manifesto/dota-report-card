## Strategy

Use Impalement Arts or Tame the Beasts to secure creep last hits, especially the ranged creep. With Tame the Beasts, ideally, you also want to hit an enemy hero while getting the last hit.

Contest the {healing-lotus} by either using your spells to push out the wave or save them to fight at the Lotus Pool.

Consider roaming mid to help secure runes for you mid laner or deny them to your opponent.

Use Impalement Arts to stack multiple camps at once.

Play behind one of your cores who is playing aggressively on the map to save them from an enemy gank with Escape Act.

Look to plant Wheel of Wonder behind an enemy hero and fear them towards it with Tame the Beasts to trigger your ultimate on them.

Your {aghanims-scepter} gives you a massive damage boost in teamfights with Impalements Arts daggers through Escape Act. Has great synergy with the Impalement Arts talents.

Purchase {aghanims-shard} once you have aether to massively reduce the damage of enemy heroes in teamfights with the miss chance. Works particularly well against invisible by revealing them, as well as illusions.

## Counter Strategy

Consider items and abilities that can dispel Escape Act or any of Ringmaster's other abilities.

Ringmaster can be a very problematic hero if the fight lasts too long. Try to kill him first and prevent him from using all his spells and save his teammates.

You can still stun and do damage to the enemy hero trapped in the Escape Act box with AoE abilities, in the same manner as Slark's ultimate.

Avoid looking at Ringmaster's Wheel of Wonder so as to not trigger it and get taunted.
