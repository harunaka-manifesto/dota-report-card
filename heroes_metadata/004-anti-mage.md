## Strategy

Keep your HP high in lane to farm creeps and pressure enemy heroes with Blink and Mana Break. Buy consumables if necessary. Get {perseverance} as soon as you can if playing a heavy harass lane.

Hit opponents and burn their mana whenever you can, for example when they go for a last hit. Find openings to punish the enemy hero when they contest creep last hits.

Play safe and acquire {battle-fury} as early as possible. Your farming speed goes up exponentially with it by spam-Blinking around jungle camps and waves.

Look around the map while you are farming to dodge ganks but also to potentially snipe a kill with Mana Void. Look for low mana and low health enemy heroes.

Plant {observer-ward} for yourself in the area you are farming to secure vision for yourself and your team.

Keep a lane pushed out to apply some pressure on the map. If you expect to be ganked, Blink into trees and {town-portal-scroll} away to the opposite side of the map.

Build items that give you tankiness such as {eye-of-skadi} and {abyssal-blade}. These items provide good overall stats and durability with damage.

Whenever your teammates die, consider cutting a wave behind the opponents or push out some other lane.

## Counter Strategy

Anti-Mage is a weak laner - so look to pressure him from the start, especially when he is level 1-3.

Look to spend your mana before it gets burned by Anti-Mage. The longer the lane goes, the more mana he will burn from you.

Against Anti-Mage, during the laning phase, buy a {magic-stick} or consider buying a {soul-ring} to have mana for spells

Look to disrupt Anti-Mage's jungling by placing deep {observer-ward} and {smoke-of-deceit} on him. Anti-Mage is weak until he gets {manta-style}.

Consider getting {boots-of-travel} on one of the cores to address Anti-Mage's split push playstyle

In team fights, look to save the hero that Anti-Mage jumps on. But be careful as you might blow up too if you get too close to a Mana Voided hero

Try to push together as a team and secure megas, since Anti-Mage is really weak in the early to mid game. Grouping up limits his farm and forces him into unfavorable fights.
