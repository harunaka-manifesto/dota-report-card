## Strategy

Use Frost Arrows frequently to harass as you don't pull creep aggro when manually casting it. Also, you can use it on creeps to secure last hits with the extra damage.

Be careful of allowing the enemies to get too close during laning stage. You are especially vulnerable when your support is pulling the lane. Keep a safe distance and learn to kite heroes down.

Multishot does the most damage when used point blank as all the arrows will hit the target. Try to cover maximal area of your targets when using the spell in the cast zone.

When in trouble, you can potentially Gust and {town-portal-scroll} out. Gust will silence and push the enemies back, allowing you time to escape.

Drow Ranger loves agility stats as they get further increased by her innate, Precision Aura. Look to spam agility items to boost your damage output, attack speed, and armor.

Drow Ranger is all about positioning. Avoid showing yourself too early in the fight and maintain distance. Let your teammates be the frontliners instead and use them as a meat shield to attack freely in fights.

Use Multishot to push waves while staying hidden in trees to avoid getting caught out when killing lane creeps. This is the only way you can clear waves without showing yourself.

When you get your {aghanims-scepter}, your single target damage increases dramatically. Your Multishot also lets you farm a lot faster.

You can move at a slower rate while channeling Multishot.

Gust lets you reveal invisible enemy heroes. They remain visible throughout the silence duration.

## Counter Strategy

Drow Ranger is slow and squishy with low armor. Look to harass her as much as you can on early levels.

Avoid being in point target range of Drow Ranger when she starts channeling Multishot

Drow Ranger's Gust and Multishot have cone shaped area of effect. If you are on top of her. look to side step, or run past, or through her when she uses those spells

Gap closing items are good against Drow Ranger as she is a glass cannon hero. Note that her Marksmanship is disabled when you are within 300 range of her

{heavens-halberd} is amazing against Drow Ranger as it disarms her for 3 seconds

Drow Ranger should be a priority target in team fights as her team loses the Precision Aura when she dies.

Use {smoke-of-deceit} to wrap around Drow Ranger and kill her.
