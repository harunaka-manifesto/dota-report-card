## Infests other units for mobility and strength

## Strategy

Right-click opponents as much as you can in the lane for extra damage and sustain from your Feast passive. Also, make sure to secure last hits and denies to get free max HP from the passive.

{orb-of-corrosion} is great option at harassing enemies in the laning stage and works well against high healing lineups.

Consider investing a point in Rage early on if there's a need for dispel or debuff immunity.

If the enemy has no way to cancel Rage and {town-portal-scroll}, be more aggresive on sides lanes and use the opportunity to Teleport out.

You can save an ally by using Infest on them, instantly granting them a health bonus.

Lifestealer doesn't farm quickly before getting his first farming item, such as {radiance}. But after that, he becomes a strong hero to fight with in the mid game.

If you have a gap-closing ally, you can choose to Infest him to be able to get on top of opponents quickly.

{orchid-malevolence}, {skull-basher} and {scythe-of-vyse} are problematic for your hero. Adjust your playstyle and itemization accordingly.

You can slow siege opponents' building by damaging it during Rage and then backing away.

Use your laning prowess to bully enemy heroes out of lane and farm towards your first major item. Lifestealer becomes very difficult to kill in lane once he reaches level 6.

## Counter Strategy

Chip damage doesn't work against Lifestealer as he is able to recover with lifesteal from Feast. If you manage to bring him below half HP, it will be hard for him to lifesteal back up as he risks dying

Buy {boots-of-speed} early against Lifestealer as he is a fast hero who often rushes {phase-boots} or {orb-of-corrosion}

Items and spells that go through debuff immunity are effective against Lifestealer

When you gank Lifestealer, he will Rage and {town-portal-scroll} out on first signs of trouble. Make sure you disable him before he Rages if you do not yet have a disable that goes through debuff immunity

Lifestealer's main spell in fights is Rage. Play around it by disabling him or kite him by saving heroes he is chasing

Lifestealer tends to have evasion due to his {radiance}. Look to counter it by getting accuracy and true strike items, like {monkey-king-bar}

Keep in mind that Lifestealer might be Infested in the initiating hero or that he can use Infest to escape
