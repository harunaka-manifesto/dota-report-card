## Five are stronger than one

By summoning up to five permanent copies of himself, Meepo can fight from every front at once. Spreading his clones throughout the battlefield, when an enemy is found, he binds his foe, gathers his selves with an **explosive teleport**, then buries what remains.

Geomancy

ABILITY: Passive

PASSIVE: Breakable

Meepo grants stacking bonuses to himself and his clones based on their terrain. Each Meepo grants his bonus to the other Meepos.

If there is a tree within 250 range, he receives 1 HP regen.

If he is standing on dirt, he receives 2% bonus movement speed.

### Divided We Stand

ABILITY: Passive

* * *

Meepo summons another Meepo, which levels up and shares items and experience with the main Meepo. Ability cooldowns are independent, but items share cooldowns between Meepos, except Teleport Scrolls.

Damage, attack speed, health, mana, regeneration, mana burn, and proc chance bonuses from items are distributed equally among the amount of Meepos, making them 50 / 66 / 75 / 80% less effective.

If any Meepo dies, they all die.

## Strategy

Control both of the runes every time it spawns by sending both your main and clone to each of the rune to prevent the enemy mid laner from taking it.

Meepo is a tempo hero that falls off over time. Hit your item timings, secure Roshan and play aggressively. Send your Clones to side lanes to gank early if there are potential kills with the Earthbind root.

If you get {diffusal-blade}, look for pick-offs as soon as you have it. Otherwise, try to hit your {consecrated-wraps} or {sange-and-yasha} power spike as early as you can by farming.

Keep side lanes pushed in and linger around with a clone to gank the opponents that show themselves to defend their tower or push out the lane.

Meepo can take Roshan down on his own fairly early. Kill first Roshan before the 20 minute mark.

## Counter Strategy

Someone should buy {spirit-vessel} against Meepo to counter healing from Ransack.

Meepo farms quickly due to Poof. Place deep {observer-ward}, gank him with {smoke-of-deceit} and block off camps with {sentry-ward}.

Look to burst one of the Meepos and don't split your damage.

Meepo takes buildings down fast. Organize defense quickly.

Meepo is snowbally hero and falls off. Don't be greedy with items and allow him to pick you off.

{crimson-guard} and armor items are great against Meepo.

Meepo is able to solo kill Roshan early on. Place {observer-ward} and check the Roshan pit.

Meepo is great at split pushing and ratting. Consider getting {boots-of-travel} on a core.
