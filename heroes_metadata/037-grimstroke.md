## Disables enemies with a swipe of his brush

Skilled at manipulating battle from the outskirts, Grimstroke studies every movement and assesses each line, deciding when to emerge from inky darkness to **disable his victims** and **bind them together** with a swipe of his brush. He delights in unleashing phantom thralls to **vex opponents**, as he carefully considers how to dispatch multiple enemies with one fateful stroke.

## Strategy

Use Stroke of Fate to secure range creep last hits and hit opponents at the same time.

Communicate whenever you want to use Ink Swell on an ally so it doesn't go to waste.

Stroke of Faith can be used to stack an additional camp from distance. You can even hit multiple camps in one cast of the spell with its curved pathing.

Focus on using Soulbind on mobile heroes or heroes with {black-king-bar}.

Buy {aghanims-shard} after you have {aether-lens} to dispel off roots, silences, and slows from your cores with Ink Swell.

{aghanims-scepter} allows you to scale well into the late game by creating illusions of high damage right clickers on the enemy team.

Use portals to roam around the map and set up kills with your control spells.

With {aghanims-shard}, you can hold Ink Swell and use it as a dispel tool to take off roots and silences from an allied hero, similar to Aphotic Shield of {abaddon}.

## Counter Strategy

Stroke of Fate does more damage the more units it hits. Play on the side of the creep wave, or try to anticipate the path of the spell and dodge it by making a sharp turn to either side.

Help remove Grimstroke's Phantom's Embrace from your team mates by attacking it.

The longer you were next to Ink Swelled unit, the longer you will be stunned.

Against Grimstroke's Soulbind, avoid staying close to your cores, especially the mobile ones.

Magic resistance and debuff immunity items are great against Grimstroke.
