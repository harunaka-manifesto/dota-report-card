## Strategy

Rush {falcon-blade} as early as possible in lane to cover up your HP and mana weaknesses while also gaining some much needed damage.

Use Echo Slash to secure the ranged creep last hit.

Look to farm instead of fighting or rotating to acquire {desolator} as early as possible. Your kill threat goes up exponentially with it.

Plant {observer-ward} for yourself in the area you are farming to secure vision for yourself and your team.

Build items that give you tankiness such as {black-king-bar} or {sange-and-yasha}. These items provide good overall stats and durability with damage.

Raptor Dance applies a basic dispel on cast and gives you a minor invulnerability period. Use it to get yourself out of roots or slows while dealing massive AoE damage.

You can stop {town-portal-scroll} and channeled spells on enemy heroes by Marking them with Raven's Veil and then targeting them with an ability or right click.

Shodo Sai can be activated to parry and mark enemy heroes when they are right clicking you, which you can proc with a follow-up ability or attack to stun them. It can give you an initiation to follow up with Falcon Rush and the rest of your spells.

Kazurai Katana can be activated to keep an enemy hero in place and do massive burst damage with the Katana stacks. This also prevents heroes from turning, which is particularly useful against certain heroes like {bristleback}.

## Counter Strategy

Look to disrupt Kez while he's jungling by placing deep {observer-ward} and ganking him with {smoke-of-deceit}.

Kez is squishy early on and needs levels to ramp up in strength. Look to pressure him on early levels.

Kez has multiple mobility spells to close the gap on your or dodge spells. Be mindful of your positioning and spell casting.

Raptor Dance applies a basic dispel on cast and provides high magic resistance for the duration. Avoid wasting spells that might get dispelled or dodged.
