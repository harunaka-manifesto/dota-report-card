## Rapidly strikes and chains his foes

**Swift and elusive**, Ember Spirit strikes all nearby enemies in **rapid succession**, and ties them up with **burning chains**. Quickly rushing across the battlefield to arrive at one of his **exploding remnants**, few can avoid, or capture, his flame.

## Strategy

You can dodge many spells (almost all projectiles) and ranged attacks with a well timed Sleight of Fist or by activating Fire Remnants.

Push out the lane with Flame Guard and Sleight of Fist to secure the water rune that's about to spawn without missing farm.

Once you have level 2 or 3 Sleight of Fist in combiniation with {orb-of-blight}, you can start spamming it on the enemy mid laner to harass.

You can place a Fire Remnant at one of the power rune spots and run to the other one to secure the rune.

When low on health or mana, you can leave a Fire Remnant behind and {town-portal-scroll} to base to regenerate and activate the Remnant to get back into the action.

You can drop and activate Fire Remnants while Sleight of Fisting.

When leaving a Fire Remnant behind, it is good to have it hidden close to a tower, neutral camp or rune spot.

Ember Spirit is great at penetrating into opponents' backline due to high mobility of Fire Remnants.

If you are struggling to reach the backline, consider an {aghanims-scepter} to increase your reach and mobility even further.

Ember Spirit is great at split pushing as he is elusive and can always have a Fire Remnant left behind to join the team.

You can cast all your spells during Sleight of Fist or while using Activate Fire Remnant, which means you can combo activate Fire Remnant and Searing Chains.

## Counter Strategy

Flame Guard is most of Ember Spirit's damage early. Pop it with magical damage or dispel it if possible.

Make sure you are controlling water and power runes against Ember Spirit as he has very little natural sustain and relies on runes for the mana to spam his spells.

Avoid clumping up against Sleight of Fist and Searing Chains spam.

Items that can root, silence or stun are key to locking down and killing an elusive Ember Spirit.

Ember Spirit is really good at split-pushing. Consider getting {boots-of-travel} on a core to match his mobility and catch him out.

As a support, if you can leave you core alone, try securing or denying the power rune from Ember Spirit

Consider buying {mekansm} and {pipe-of-insight} against Ember Spirit to mitigate and heal through a lot of his magic damage
