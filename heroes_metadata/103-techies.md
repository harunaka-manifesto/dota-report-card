## Strategy

Use spells to quickly push out the wave and secure the rune that's about to spawn without missing farm.

Ferry some health consumables with your courier to use Blast Off! frequently.

Blast Off! destroys trees which can be useful against certain heroes and when opponents try to hide in trees.

Look to place a couple of Proximity Mines around portals for a surprise burst on enemies using them.

You can make it harder for opponents to destroy Proximity Mines by sitting on top of them.

Drop or backpack items that provide you with mana before using {arcane-boots}, {bottle} or {soul-ring}.

Sticky Bombs and Proximity Mines provide a little vision so you can scout cliffs and highgrounds, runes or the Roshan pit.

Consider {smoke-of-deceit} on yourself and moving to the opponents' side of the map to mine aggressively. Bring an {observer-ward} as well.

Look for pickoffs or join teamfights with your massive burst potential.

If your teammates are pressuring, move with them, Mine around them and Blast Off! when opponents clump up.

Place a couple of Proximity Mines at the entrance of Roshan pit to scout enemy movement.

Pick up {aghanims-shard} to be able to plant the invisible M.A.D. barrel and detonate it manually for another source of burst damage.

Consider using {force-staff} on enemy heroes to push them into Proximity Mines.

Play aggressively in lanes and the enemy jungle with your level 7 as you have massive kill potential.

With {ethereal-blade}, you can burst down just about any hero on the map with your combo.

## Counter Strategy

Against Techies, deliver extra health consumables and an {observer-ward} to keep track of him.

Proximity Mines explode 1 seconds after you hear the beep. Destroy them or move away quickly.

Keep track of Techies' movements and which part of the map is being Mined.

Avoid walking up highground or in Roshan pit without vision. You might run into mines.

Magic resistance items, {black-king-bar} and {aeon-disk} are great against Techies to avoid getting bursted by his spell combo.

Make it tricky for Techies to land Sticky Bombs on you by making sharp turns.

Avoid running too close to Techies' Minefield sign once he has {aghanims-scepter} as it triggers a massive damage area for anyone who walks through it.

Whenever Techies dies, he will leave behind a barrel in his place that will explode after 1.5 seconds. Try to run out of its AoE before it does damage
