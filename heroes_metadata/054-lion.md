## Steals enemy mana to fuel his own abilities

Lion gives enemies little chance to retaliate. **Stunning targets** with a damaging row of spikes, or temporarily **transforming them into a harmless critter**, even if his powerful spells aren't enough, he and his allies should still have all the time they need to slay their foes.

## Strategy

Use Earth Spike strategically so that you can get extra right-clicks in, deny a creep or secure the ranged creep.

You can skill Hex on level 2 to score a kill.

Hex and Mana Drain destroy basic illusions instantly.

Make a move whenever you have Finger of Death available.

When Finger of Death is on cooldown, use the time to farm your {blink-dagger}.

Initiate on enemies with your long lasting disables, so your allies can follow-up for a kill.

Fight around good vision, so you can land multi-hero Earth Spikes.

{aghanims-shard} is good at countering illusion-based heroes and lets you avoid most disables and stand your ground against heavy magic and pure damage from enemy heroes.

Keep an eye on the mana pool of allies around you and top them up with Mana Drain if they are low on mana.

Position yourself to get an uninterrupted Mana Drain as a follow up to Earth Spike to harass enemy heroes from a distance and take away their ability to fight back or last hit with their spells in lane.

## Counter Strategy

Use fog of war to cancel Lion's Mana Drain

Opt for anti-magic damage items against Lion

Be aware of Lion's level 6 Finger of Death. {infused-raindrops} might help you to survive the burst

{lotus-orb} is an effective item against Lion as all of his spells are targetable

Look to target Lion in team fights as he provides a lot of control and magic damage against your cores and is also a very squishy hero

Be careful of Lion's {blink-dagger} timing. You can usually track it, as it mostly comes between the 14 and 18 minute mark, depending on how good of a game he has.
