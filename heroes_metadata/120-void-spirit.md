## Steps in and out of the mortal plane to strike his foes

Privy to secrets that would shatter a mortal mind, the Void Spirit emerges from his Hidden Temple to stand guard over **multiple areas of the battlefield** at once. Able to step into and out of the mortal plane at will, he easily **outmaneuvers opponents** to strike from wherever he desires, calling upon the powers of the aether to **shield him** in times of need.

## Strategy

Use Resonant Pulse to secure creeps, especially the ranged creep and harass opponents at the same time. Try to follow up with some right clicks on the enemy hero afterwards.

On tough lanes, put more points in Dissimilate to be able push out waves and clear camps faster.

Look to play actively, especially with good runes as Void Spirit has huge burst potential with the low cooldown spells.

Void Spirit is great at killing supports and backliners in teamfights. Focus on those first.

When not much is happening, push out the side lanes with your AoE damage spells.

{aghanims-scepter} is a game changer as it adds another utility in the form of silence on Resonant Pulse.

Once you have {aghanims-scepter}, look to play aggressively as you can solo kill pretty much any hero on the map.

Pick up shard to farm creeps with Aether Remnant and let it have a better chance of catching enemy heroes as well as reveal invisible enemies.

## Counter Strategy

Control power runes against Void Spirit. He likes to bottle them and gank.

Instant disables and silences are great against the elusive Void Spirit.

Magic resistance and debuff immunity items are great against Void Spirit.

Be aware of Void Spirit's {aghanims-scepter} timing as he gets AoE silence of 3.5 seconds.
