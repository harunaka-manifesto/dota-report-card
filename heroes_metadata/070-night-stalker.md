## Strategy

Run to mid as you load into the game and use your night time vision to scout for opponents' mid laner placing {observer-ward}.

Night Stalker has great night vision but very limited day vision.

Look to survive through the first 5 minutes of the game and ideally have {phase-boots} as the night hits.

Use Void to secure ranged creep last hits.

Try to manipulate creep lane equilibrium to get it closer to your tower during the day time.

Keep a skill point or opt to skill Crippling Fear as the first night approaches if the silence is necessary to score a kill.

Play and farm aggressively during the night time and provide additional vision for your team.

When going for a kill, consider saving Void for the ministun, if the enemy tries to {town-portal-scroll} out.

Avoid fighting when it's day time and Dark Ascension is on cooldown - play defensively or push out lanes.

Prioritize dealing with backliners and supports in fights who struggle to get out of your Crippling Fear AoE, especially once you have {blink-dagger}.

## Counter Strategy

Night Stalker is weak in the first 5 minutes of the game. Pressure him early.

Night Stalker will fight you at the 5 minute mark, as night time will hit.

Night Stalker's flying vision during Dark Ascension will allow him to see you inside or behind trees.

Look to fight during day time and when Dark Ascension is down.

{force-staff} and {hurricane-pike} are great items against Night Stalker's Crippling Fear.

Break effects remove Hunter in the Night and Night Stalker becomes significantly weaker.

Look to apply pressure on Night Stalker before he reaches his level 6 during day time.
