## Launches a powerful icy blast from anywhere on the map

Able to launch a powerful blast of **damaging ice** across the battlefield, Ancient Apparition threatens to shatter weakened enemies wherever they are. Slowing enemies as he enhances his allies' effectiveness in battle, he is a constant threat to his foes.

## Strategy

Make maximum use of your high attack range and Chilling Touch to harass in the lane without aggroing lane creeps.

Use Cold Feet when it is likely to stun, for example, after your laning partner applies a disable or slow, or just before the opponent wants to last hit a creep.

Ice Vortex can be used to provide vision on top of pillars or in Roshan pit.

Prioritize Ice Blasting heroes high on healing and with big health pool.

Grab {aghanims-shard} at 15 minute mark as it gives you a massive AoE stun with Ice Blast.

When not much is happening on the map, use Ice Vortex to farm creep waves without showing yourself.

Block the enemy pull camp with your body or a {sentry-ward}.

If the lane is pushed out, try pulling the small neutral camp into your lane creeps to regain lane control and farm the neutrals at the same time

Keep an eye on the minimap and look for opportunities to steal the Wisdom shrine when enemies are distracted or dead. This can give your team an XP advantage and deny the enemy support's level timing.

## Counter Strategy

When Ancient Apparition's Cold Feet is used on you, look to break it with a mobility spell or walking away before you get stunned.

Ancient Apparition's Ice Blast negates all healing. Avoid using healing items when affected by it.

Ice Blast goes through debuff immunity but is offset by magic resistance and magic barrier items.

Do not clump up against Ancient Apparition because if his ultimate lands on your team, it can completely turn the fight against you
