## Strategy

Spam Sticky Napalm on the enemy mid laner to keep them low and slowed at all times. You need not worry about giving {magic-stick} charges.

Spam Sticky Napalm on the enemy heroes to keep them low and slowed at all times. Doing so from fog in tree lines prevents them from building up {magic-stick} charges.

Apply Sticky Napalm on opponents but also on the creeps at the same time in order to last hit. Batrider's base damage is very low.

Use spells to quickly push out the wave and secure the rune that's about to spawn without missing farm.

When you have 6 Sticky Napalm stacks on an opponent, you can consider going for a kill attempt.

Having {bottle} charges, {faerie-fire} or {healing-salve} can allow you to dive under the tower.

Batrider is good at clearing stacks early on. Encourage your teammates to stack camps for you.

Pick up {aghanims-shard} in the mid to late game to remove the stack limit on Sticky Napalm and let it do damage to buildings.

## Counter Strategy

{magic-stick} and {magic-wand} are core lane items against Batrider's Sticky Napalm spam.

If you have 6 or more stacks of Sticky Napalm, be careful or back away to allow them to expire.

{lotus-orb} and {linkens-sphere} are good at countering Batrider's Flaming Lasso.

Once Batrider has {aghanims-scepter}, maintain distance from your allies as Flaming Lasso will grab you as well if you are within 650 range of the Lasso target.
