## Strategy

Make sure pull camps are not blocked as the lane will be pushing frequently due to Rip Tide. Ask your support to start with a {sentry-ward}.

Use Mirror Image and send out your illusions to scout the opponents' position safely.

Mirror Image illusions can be used to deny runes or to deceive the opponents about your hero's presence.

Use Mirror Image non stop to last hit and trade with your opponents.

Mirror Image applies basic dispel on cast and provides half a second of invulnerability.

Consider putting a point in Ensnare around level 3 but only if you can guarantee a kill with it.

Setup hotkeys and control groups to easily control and split up your illusions.

Naga Siren is not a particularly strong laner. If you find it difficult to farm in lane, you can move into the jungle as early as level 4.

Once you get {yasha} and become a little stronger, keep 1 illusion with you to farm and send the other 2 to push out a wave or farm another camp at the same time.

Keep sending out your illusions to push lanes or even farm creep camps in the opponent's jungle while you farm safely in yours.

Be quick at judging the situation and pop Song of the Siren if you're getting ganked or want to setup for your team to follow up.

You can use Song of the Siren to secure Roshan kill.

{aghanims-scepter} is a great pick up to control debuff immune heroes.

## Counter Strategy

Clear Naga Siren's Illusions in lane as they are squishy but provide her with significant damage. This would cripple her last hitting and trading capabilities.

Hex, both from spells and {scythe-of-vyse}, as well as {dagon} destroy Mirror Image illusions instantly.

Naga Siren farms quickly with Mirror Image illusions. Place deep {observer-ward}, block camps with {sentry-ward} and gank her with {smoke-of-deceit} .

Items that provide AoE damage, especially magical damage, are great at dealing with Mirror Image illusions.

{crimson-guard} and armor items are great against Naga Siren's physical damage.

Naga Siren is great at split pushing and ratting. Consider getting {boots-of-travel} on a core to match her map presence.

Naga Siren can pop Song of the Siren and hit Roshan as he does not get affected by it. Be mindful about this when taking or contesting Roshan.
