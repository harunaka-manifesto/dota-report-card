## Strategy

Catch both opponents on the lane with Decay and use the extra damage to harass and secure last hits.

Check for the number of {magic-stick} charges opponents have before diving or committing for a kill.

Don't make it too easy for opponents to destroy Tombstone. Place it on pillars, cliffs and protect it.

Tombstone destroys trees and provides great vision.

When you drop low on resources, consider suiciding into neutral camp or opponents towers to reset quickly.

During Flesh Golem, look to right click an enemy hero who your cores are targeting at least once for damage amplification and spawn a zombie on them.

Undying allows the team to kill Roshan early due to zombie tanking and damage amplification from Flesh Golem.

Play as aggressively as you can in front of your team with Flesh Golem and Decay to absorb damage and chase down enemy heroes. Don't be afraid of dying.

Keep in mind to use your {aghanims-shard} defensively on an allied hero and save them from being bursted.

## Counter Strategy

Undying uses Decay frequently. Make sure you buy {magic-stick} or {magic-wand} against him.

Keep distance from Undying as he is likely to have high damage due to Decay stacks.

Keep an eye on your total HP against Decay spam. Soul Rip can finish you off.

Make Tombstone a priority in fights or you risk being slowed greatly and take a lot of damage.

Consider killing off zombies chasing you or team mates in trouble.

Avoid being hit by Undying in Flesh Golem form as you will take extra damage, have a zombie chasing you, and be slowed.
