## Strategy

Double Ion Shell the first wave and make sure opponents don't deny many creeps. Ask your teammate on lane to pull the opponent's wave for max efficiency.

Execute push and pull strategy. Push the lane with Ion Shell and pull the big camp.

If you can't pressure the enemy heroes in lane, particularly the enemy core, consider creep skipping and farming neutral camps nearby.

If you are fighting close to pillar spots or cliffs, consider Vacuuming opponents on those.

Push out dangerous lanes with Ion Shell on your creeps instead of walking up with your hero.

Keep your melee cores Ion Shelled and wait for a moment when opponents are clumped up to do your combo.

Prioritize using Wall of Replica and Normal Punch on heroes that have strong illusions or auras.

Utilize your innate ability on lane - gaining levels gives you free health and mana; so look to fight around those power spikes. If the lane is going well, skip consumables to accelerate your core items.

Level 3 is your first power spike. If you have a melee ally in lane, consider casting Ion Shell on both yourself and your teammate. This allows you to play aggressively and pressure the enemy with double shell damage.

## Counter Strategy

Against Dark Seer, focus on denying creeps as Ion Shell is making them drop low.

Dark Seer will push the lane in with Ion Shell and pull the big neutral camp. Block it or prevent it.

Dark Seer relies on {clarity} to maintain Ion Shell spam. Look to cancel them.

When Dark Seer's Surge is on cooldown, he is vulnerable.

Avoid buying {radiance} and {assault-cuirass} against Dark Seer as he can replicate these items on illusions with his ultimate.

Don't clump up in teamfights for Vacuum into Wall of Replica combination of Dark Seer.
