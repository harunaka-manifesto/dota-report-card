## Strategy

Use Shadow Poison from fog to not fill {magic-stick} charges for opponents.

Don't spam Shadow Poison unless you have mana and a chance to build up 5 stacks.

You can Disrupt an opponent's core and deny a creep or two.

Shadow Poison is great for stacking multiple camps.

Demonic Purge is great for kiting debuff immune heroes and to dispel important buffs, including runes.

Don't show yourself at the start of the fight. Be quick at saving an ally with Disruption.

When not much is happening, push out the side lanes from the fog with Shadow Poison.

You can use Disruption on your cores to push out waves or to make deception plays.

{aghanims-scepter} is a must in late game for kiting opponents and for break effect.

Pick up {aghanims-shard} with {blink-dagger} to dispel debuffs off yourself or an allied hero and prevent them getting kited or bursted down under roots and silences.

## Counter Strategy

Shadow Demon uses Shadow Poison frequently to harass. {magic-stick} or {magic-wand} will be charged up.

If you have 3 stacks of Shadow Poison, back up a bit and don't let yourself get hit by it again.

Shadow Demon's Demonic Purge applies continuous dispel and removes positive buffs from spells and items.

Focus Shadow Demon in the fights as he provides saves and control for his team.

Demonic Purge slows and damages through debuff immunity. Be careful not to waste {black-king-bar} on it.

Avoid buying {radiance} and {assault-cuirass} against Disruption as it will replicate these items.

Status resistance items, {linkens-sphere} and a well timed {lotus-orb} are great against Shadow Demon.

Once Shadow Demon gets {aghanims-scepter}, he's able to break passive spells with Demonic Purge.
