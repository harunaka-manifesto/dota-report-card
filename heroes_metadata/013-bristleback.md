### Bristleback

ABILITY: Passive

PASSIVE: Breakable

* * *

Bristleback takes less damage if hit on the sides or rear. If Bristleback takes enough damage from the rear, he releases a Quill Spray of the current level.

* * *

Bristleback's rear is considered to be within 70 degrees from the back.

Bristleback's side is considered to be within 110 degrees from the back.

Damage done by Quill Sprays released by this ability passively is considered reflected damage. Reflected damage does not provide any lifesteal and cannot be reflected.

* * *

**SIDE DAMAGE REDUCTION:** 8% / 12% / 16% / 20%

**BACK DAMAGE REDUCTION:** 16% / 24% / 32% / 40%

**DAMAGE THRESHOLD:** 275 / 250 / 225 / 200

## Strategy

If you can be aggressive in lane, take a point in Viscous Nasal Goo as it will greatly improve physical damage output when paired with your support in lane.

Turn your back to opponents, towers or stacks of neutral creeps whenever you expect to take loads of damage. It greatly reduces the amount of damage received and procs your Quill Spray.

Alert your supports to stack for you, especially ancient camps as you can clear it at level 7. This will give you the ability to scale fast into the game compared to everyone else.

Once you get a tanky item like {consecrated-wraps}, {blade-mail}, {lotus-orb}, or {vanguard}, play very aggressively in lane. Look to push the enemy out of the lane and break their safe lane tower with the help of your support.

Check opponents' inventories frequently in search for break effects and healing reductions. {black-king-bar} and dispel items counter some of them.

You can take Roshan down fast and early with your team thanks to Viscous Nasal Goo. Abuse these openings for an early power spike into a high ground push off the Aegis.

Bristleback's lack of disables can allow enemies to {town-portal-scroll} away rather than try and fight him. A {skull-basher} or {euls-scepter-of-divinity} does well to counter that.

## Counter Strategy

{magic-stick} and {magic-wand} are must items against Bristleback's Quill Spray spam. He will use it to secure last hits so you will be able to get many charges on your Magic Stick or Wand.

Someone should buy {spirit-vessel} against Bristleback to counter his regeneration and healing. This makes him drastically squishier in the mid-late game phase.

If you have no good way of dealing with Bristleback, ignore him and don't waste too much resources on him. Deal with his teammates instead.

{silver-edge} allows you to break Bristleback's passives. This is extremely important to make him squishy by breaking down his tankiness in fights.

Armor items are good to counter Bristleback's physical damage output.

Buy {shivas-guard} and {crellas-crozier} to counter Bristleback's high HP regen and deal with him

If Bristleback is chasing you alone, it's better to just {town-portal-scroll} out.
