## Strategy

Secure ranged creeps with Fade Bolt and hit opponents at the same time.

When fighting next to pillars or a cliff, consider using Telekinesis to leave an enemy hero stranded on top of it.

In the mid game, get hold of a wave clearing spell when the game slows down to be able to farm and kill creep waves.

When not much is happening, push out side lanes with your Fade Bolt and a stolen wave clearing spell.

Be patient in fights with Spell Steal and make sure to get a high impact spell.

Spam your Fade Bolt by using it on the first target you see, rather than keeping it for a KS.

Use your level 4 Fade Bolt on the ranged creep first to one shot it while killing creep waves. But make sure the melee creeps are in range for the spell to bounce on them afterwards.

Look to inform your teammates when you cast abilities like Supernova or Macropyre so they dont get confused or run away from the AoE.

Fade Bolt reduces both right click and spell damage on the affected heroes. Use it to trade effectively with enemy heroes while they cannot dish out their maximum damage in the debuff duration.

## Counter Strategy

Avoid picking heroes with huge ultimates against Rubick such as {enigma}, {magnus} and {tidehunter}

Avoid trading hits with Rubick while under effect of Fade Bolt as your attack and spell damage are reduced

Rubick's Telekinesis can be broken by using {force-staff} on the affected ally

Be mindful about spell usage so that you don't give away good spells to Rubick. Rubick is generally looking for stuns and high damage abilities

{lotus-orb} is an effective item against Rubick as his damage and control spells are targetable.

Look to target Rubick in fights as he is a very squishy hero. If you manage to kill him, you don't have to worry about spell casting anymore
