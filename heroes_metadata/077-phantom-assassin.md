The moment she finds her prey, Phantom Assassin strikes. Instantly closing in on a target, she effortlessly **dodges attacks** as she relentlessly cuts away at her foe. Without warning, any one of her attacks could prove suddenly and **brutally fatal.**

## Strategy

Use Stifling Dagger to secure creep last hits, especially the ranged creep.

Try to get to {perseverance} early in tough lanes where you play passively and then get {power-treads} afterwards to continue jungling faster. Send out extra HP regen if necessary to survive the lane.

Opponents are likely to have a lot of {magic-stick} charges; so take that into account when playing aggressively.

You can disjoint most projectile spells and ranged attacks with Blur.

When farming dangerous areas or stacks, consider using Blur as the creeps won't attack you back.

You typically want to focus on killing disabling supports and backliners at the start of the fight. Try to catch them from Blur.

Check opponents' inventories for break effects and evasion-piercing items. Adjust playstyle and itemization accordingly.

Pick up {aghanims-scepter} if you need the break vs heroes like {timbersaw}, {bristleback}, etc.

## Counter Strategy

Phantom Assassin uses Stifling Dagger frequently to get last hits. Buy {magic-stick} and {magic-wand} to get charges.

Armor items are good against Phantom Assassin as she is all about physical damage.

Cheap items that allow you to survive Phantom Assassin's Phantom Strike are {ghost-scepter}, {glimmer-cape}, and {euls-scepter-of-divinity}.

Consider purchasing items that pierce through evasion, breaks passives or causes magical damage to counter Phantom Assassin's passive evasion ability.

{blade-mail} will be a big nuisance for Phantom Assassin until she has {black-king-bar}.

Phantom Assassin is weak until she gets {black-king-bar} - try to {smoke-of-deceit} and gank her before that timing and plant deep {observer-ward} in the jungle to do so.
