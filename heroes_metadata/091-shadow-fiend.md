### Requiem of Souls

DAMAGE TYPE: Magical

PIERCES DEBUFF IMMUNITY: No

PASSIVE: No

DISPELLABLE: Yes

* * *

Shadow Fiend gathers up to 20 of his captured souls to release them as lines of demonic energy. Units near Shadow Fiend when the souls are released can be damaged by several lines of energy. Any unit damaged by Requiem of Souls will be feared and have its movement speed and magic resistance reduced for 0.6 seconds for each line hit up to a maximum of 2.15. Lines of energy are created for every soul captured through Necromastery.

Requiem of Souls is automatically cast whenever Shadow Fiend dies, regardless of its cooldown.

* * *

One line is generated from Shadow Fiend for every **1** soul in his possession.

Fear and movement slow do not apply if the Requiem was released on death.

## Strategy

Stack Shadowraze debuffs on the enemy laner and secure last hits at the same time if possible.

Build up Necromastery stacks through last hitting and capitalize on the extra damage you have.

Try to stack camps close to mid after you push in the wave.

Kill the creep wave prior to rune spawns quickly and collect the runes. You need runes badly for mana.

Alert your teammates to stack for you as you can clear those early.

Look to farm your {black-king-bar} before you join fights as Shadow Fiend is quite susceptible to disables.

Keep side lanes pushed out with Shadowraze when not much is happening on the map.

## Counter Strategy

Shadow Fiend has low starting damage and he relies on gathering souls through last hits. Try to contest last hits with him when his base damage is low.

Shadow Fiend does not have an escape mechanism - so look to gank him during the laning stage. If left unchallenged, he's one of the best farming heroes

Try not to run at Shadow Fiend in plain sight as you might get triple razed to death

Keep an eye out on the availability of {black-king-bar} in Shadow Fiend's inventory and try to gank him continuously before he gets the item.

In teamfights, look to play around Shadow Fiend's ultimate by either canceling it or moving away from him

Armor items are good against Shadow Fiend as his Presence of the Dark Lord is an armor reducing aura

Shadow Fiend should be a high priority target in team fights. If left unchecked, he can do tons of damage. At the same time, he's not that tanky and easy to bring down
