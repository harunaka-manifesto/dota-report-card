## Strategy

Use Storm Hammer to secure ranged creep last hit and damage the opponents at the same time.

Alert your teammates to stack for you as you can clear those fairly early with points in Great Cleave. Prioritize ancient creeps once you get your {mask-of-madness}.

Only put points in Great Cleave once you are ready to leave the lane as it makes the lane push out from the Cleave damage. Or keep it at one point in lane until you are ready to leave the lane.

Don't overstay in lane. Consider moving out of the lane as laning opponents are getting closer to level 6.

Feel free to pop God's Strength off cooldown to farm after laning phase.

Your initial jump in the fights has to be well timed and precise. You don't want to be kited.

In teamfights, position yourself in such a way so that you can Cleave onto the opponents further away.

Itemize against kiting by purchasing {black-king-bar}, {blink-dagger}, {harpoon}, {shadow-blade}, or {aghanims-scepter}.

## Counter Strategy

Against Sven, avoid playing inside the creep wave as you will take damage from Great Cleave.

Sven farms quickly with Great Cleave. {smoke-of-deceit} on him, place deep {observer-ward} for vision and {sentry-ward} to block his camps.

God's Strength more than doubles Sven's damage. Look to disengage whilst its active.

Avoid clumping up in the fights for Storm Hammer and Great Cleave.

Armor items are great against Sven's insane physical damage output.

Be aware of Sven's {harpoon} timing. He gets another initiation tool.
