## Strategy

If the lane is tough, consider putting more points in Plague Wards and move to the jungle if necessary.

Hit opponents regularly to apply ticking damage from Poison Sting.

Galed opponents can be denied, so do your best to secure a last hit on a dying hero.

Deny low health Plague Wards to deprive opponents of extra gold.

Venomancer is great at defending towers as you can place a bunch of Plague Wards around it.

Use Plague Wards off cooldown when you are moving around to scout, control runes and block camps.

Farm aggressively, as Plague Wards provide vision and farm dangerous spots for you along with the kill threat of Venomous Gale and Noxious Plague.

In fights, look to affect as many heroes as possible with Venomous Gale.

When not much is happening, push out the side lanes with Venomous Gale and Plague Wards.

Pick up {aghanims-scepter} in the mid game to significantly increase your damage in teamfights with the Noxious Plague spread and the magic resistance reduction on affected enemy heroes.

Noxious Plague will snowball a teamfight in your favour if you can burst down the first hero affected by it.

## Counter Strategy

Bring extra consumables to the lane against Venomancer's Poison Sting harass.

{shawl} and {cloak} have a lot of value against Venomancer's insane magic damage output.

Consider {town-portal-scroll} out quickly when you find yourself in trouble against Venomancer.

Debuff immunity and magic resistance items are great against Venomancer.

Items that provide dispels are able to remove Venomous Gale, Snakebite, and Poison Sting.
