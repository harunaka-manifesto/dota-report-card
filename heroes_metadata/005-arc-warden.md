## Creates a copy of himself to split push

## Strategy

Make use of Spark Wraith to secure creep last hits, especially the ranged creeps.

If an opponent splits away from creeps or his ally, you can Flux him and follow up with right clicks and Spark Wraith.

Use Spark Wraith to scout the opponents and protect yourself while farming.

Make sure not to feed Tempest Double to opponents as it gives away a lot of gold and experience.

Use Tempest Double as frequently as possible to split push the side lanes and damage buildings.

Avoid being part of the teamfights from the very start until you get first major item that will tank you up.

Whenever opponents Teleport back on a hero or two to address your split push, look to fight the remaining opponents, {smoke-of-deceit} or take Roshan.

Use Magnatic Field to secure runes every power rune if you are playing mid lane. Each rune permanently boosts your stats. You can even secure both water runes with a well placed Magnetic Field to take one and pick the other with your hero.

## Counter Strategy

Arc Warden's Flux does no damage to you if you are close to another unit.

Arc Warden's Spark Wraith takes time to charge which gives you an opportunity to run away and dodge it.

Units inside Arc Warden's Magnetic Field can be hit if you are inside of it or have evasion piercing items.

Arc Warden is a weak laner. Pressure him early on.

Arc Warden's Tempest Double gives a lot of gold and experience. Kill it if possible.

Arc Warden farms the jungle a lot. Plant deep {observer-ward} and gank him. Also block off camps with {sentry-ward}.

Buy gap closing items to get on top of Arc Warden.

{boots-of-travel} allow to defend against Arc Warden's split-pushing and to quickly rejoin the team.

Once Arc Warden has {aghanims-scepter}, look to walk close to one of your allies or creeps when targeted by Flux to prevent the silence and damage.
