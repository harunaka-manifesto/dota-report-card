## Strategy

Searing Arrows is your main tool for harassing in the lane - try to use it as often as you can on the enemy heroes without even drawing creep agro.

Check opponent's inventory frequently for detection.

Death Pact gives you a massive boost of current and max HP. Look to maintain at least one charge of it to use during fights.

Once you have {desolator}, you can look for kills and get involved in fights.

Avoid passing by the typical {sentry-ward} spots like pillars and active rune spots.

In fights, try to focus targets that you can burst down quickly, such as supports or squishy heroes without escapes.

Use the {daedalus} power spike to put massive pressure on the map by getting kill after kill on enemy heroes and towers.

Pick up {aghanims-shard} after getting {desolator} to have AoE damage in teamfights as well as instant wave clear with Burning Barrage.

Contest or steal wisdom runes every 7 mins with the Skeleton Walk invisibility. You are strong in a 1v1 against most enemy heroes.

With {aghanims-shard}, remember to toggle Searing Arrows to auto-cast before using Burning Barrage to do more damage.

## Counter Strategy

Bring a {sentry-ward} to the lane and {dust-of-appearance} later on for Clinkz.

Try to go on Clinkz when his death pact expires or if he is too far forward.

Make sure to always carry {dust-of-appearance} vs Clinkz and use {sentry-ward} on the popular fighting/farming spots.

Pair {observer-and-sentry-wards} to spot Clinkz's movements.

Carry detection on multiple heroes against Clinkz.

Armor items are good against Clinkz.
