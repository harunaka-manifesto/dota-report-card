## Poisonous toxins chip away armor and slow enemies

Launching **deadly poison from a distance**, Viper poses a grave threat to whomever he targets. As his various toxins **eat away at an enemy's health** and **slow their movement**, Viper simply does what he does best: he keeps adding more poison.

## Strategy

Viper is very strong in early to mid game but drops off later on. Play actively while you're the strongest.

Look to stack up Poison Attacks on a single target and fix your mana issues early on.

Viper is insanely slow because of his low movement speed. Consider getting a casual {wind-lace} and {boots-of-travel} in the long run.

You can kill tanky heroes who rely on their passive abilities with the break effect from your Viper Strike.

Push out the side lanes when not much is happening on the map. You can do it from fog with Nethertoxin.

Although you are very tanky in early stages, you aren't nearly as tanky around the 20 minute mark without items that give you HP like {consecrated-wraps} and {aghanims-scepter}.

Pick up {aghanims-shard} later on to further increase your DPS and building damage.

## Counter Strategy

Don't allow Viper to stack more than 3 Poison Attacks on you.

Viper Strike breaks passives. Stay away from it until you have a {black-king-bar}.

Consider {town-portal-scroll} out quickly when you find yourself in trouble against Viper.

Magic resistance items are great against Viper's magic damage.

{linkens-sphere} and a well timed {lotus-orb} are great against Viper Strike.
