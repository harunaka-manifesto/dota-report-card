## Strategy

Skywrath Mage is very squishy, so be mindful about your positioning.

Use Arcane Bolt or Concussive Shot to secure ranged creep last hits.

Cast your spells from fog whenever you can if opponents have {magic-stick}.

Find a ganking partner that can set up your Mystic Flare and burn {smoke-of-deceit} with him.

In fights, focus on dropping Mystic Flare on a disabled unit and apply Ancient Seal on a target with no dispel.

## Counter Strategy

Skywrath Mage uses his spells frequently to harass. {magic-stick} and {magic-wand} will be charged up.

Consider purchasing {infused-raindrops} against Skywrath Mage's damaging spells.

Focus Skywrath Mage in fights as he does a lot of damage but is very squishy.

Be aware of {rod-of-atos} timing on Skywrath Mage. He can solo kill most of the heroes with it.

{force-staff} is great at saving an ally from Skywrath Mage's Mystic Flare.

Magic resistance and debuff immunity items are great against Skywrath Mage.

Items that provide dispel are great against Skywrath Mage's Ancient Seal and the root from {rod-of-atos}.
