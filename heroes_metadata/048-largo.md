## Strategy

Use Catchy Lick to secure ranged creep last hits when you or your lane partner cannot safely walk up to it.

Use Catchy Lick to pull an enemy hero out of position, particularly when the enemy creep wave is dead. You can also use it to save an ally when they are in trouble.

Use Frogstomp and Catchy Lick together to keep an enemy hero in the stun and damage AoE for longer.

Use Catchy Lick to do a lot of damage even at early levels and pull an enemy hero closer to your melee attack range to trade with them.

Catchy Lick applies a basic dispel on cast. Use it on an ally or yourself when targeted by dispellable spells and items. Dispelling a debuff also applies a heal over time. You can also use it aggressively to take off dispellable buffs on enemy heroes.

Remember to use Croak of Genius on yourself before casting your spell combo to do more damage and conserve mana.

Look to apply Croak of Genius on an ally who does a lot of nuke damage with their spells, such as {zeus} and {lina}. It causes their spells to do damage over time and also reduces their mana cost.

Remember that you cannot use your base spells like Catchy Lick or attack while strumming your instrument during Amphibian Rhapsody. You will have to turn off your ultimate when you wish to use your base toolkit.

Look to play a bit greedy and farm your {aghanims-scepter} before rotating too much as it makes you extremely potent in teamfights.

Until you tank up with items like {glimmer-cape} and {guardian-greaves}, prioritize staying back and saving allies with Catchy Lick or provide lockdown with Frogstomp rather than standing next to your front line ally and channeling Amphibian Rhapsody.

When strumming your instrument during Amphibian Rhapsody, prioritize the healing from Island Elixir or the mobility from Hotfeet Hustle. You can do and provide damage with Bullbelly Blitz when you or your allies are in no danger whatsoever.

Remember that during Amphibian Rhapsody, you will have to strum Bullbelly Blitz to do AoE damage, even when you have {aghanims-scepter} and get to play two tunes at once.

Pick up {lotus-orb} and {glimmer-cape} with {aghanims-shard} to apply the item buff to yourself as well whenever you cast it on an ally.

Purchase {aghanims-scepter} to make yourself extremely potent in teamfights with your AoE damage, and provide buffs for allies by strumming multiple spells during Amphibian Rhapsody at the same time.

## Counter Strategy

Someone should buy {spirit-vessel} to nullify Largo's healing.

Be aware of your positioning and do not get pulled out of position by Largo's Catchy Lick.

Largo's Catchy Lick applies a basic dispel. He can take off dispellable buffs from enemies as well as debuff his allies.

{orchid-malevolence} and other control items are a good counter to stop Largo from using his high impact spells in teamfights.

Items that offset healing such as {eye-of-skadi} and {crellas-crozier} good against Largo.

Nullifier's continuous dispel heavily counters Largo and takes off the buffs from Croak of Genius, Bullbelly Blitz and Hotfeet Hustle.
