## Strategy

Treants are not only good for last hitting or harassing but also to scout, block off camps and deny runes.

Consider body blocking opponents with Treants when going for a kill or trying to escape.

When low on health, consider using {town-portal-scroll} to go back to base and Teleport back with the spell.

Wisdom shrines spawn at minute 7, look to steal the enemy one with Teleport as it gives you a lot and denies a lot from the enemy.

Look around the map for opportunities to gank, take towers, snipe couriers, pick up runes or place deep {observer-ward}.

Sprout provides vision in the area and can be useful to scout pillars for {observer-ward} or Roshan.

Save yourself or an ally by placing Sprout strategically. It is great for kiting debuff-immune opponents.

Sprout can push you up or down the cliff or pillar.

Typically, use Wrath of Nature on the opposite side of the map from where you want it to hit hard.

When not much is happening, push out the side lanes with your Sprout and summons.

Nature's Prophet excels at ratting, so resort to it if the game slows down or you fall behind.

Use Sprout and summons to safely clear creep waves in dangerous areas of the map where you might die.

## Counter Strategy

Nature's Prophet is great at sniping couriers. Fly courier over trees when possible

To counter Nature Prophet's Sprout, buy {quelling-blade}, {tango} or {force-staff}.

Use a {tango} to get out of Sprout trees if you do not have a {quelling-blade}.

Wrath of Nature can hit you with up to 400 damage at level 6.

If Wrath of Nature hits you and there are no enemies around, then you are close to an {observer-ward}.

Nature's Prophet can take down buildings fast. Organize defense quickly.

Nature's Prophet is great at split-pushing and ratting. Consider getting {boots-of-travel} on a core.

Consider buying a {dagon} on one or more heroes if Nature's Prophet took the health and damage talent for his Treants on level 25 to kill them.
