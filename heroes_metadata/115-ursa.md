## Strategy

Ursa has low base damage, so you might want to hit the creep once or twice to apply Fury Swipes before last hitting.

You can jump over cliffs or into treelines with Earthshock.

Ursa typically farms very slowly before {battle-fury} so you need to play actively and look for kills to gain net worth.

You can take Roshan on your own fairly early due to Fury Swipes stacks. Get a {smoke-of-deceit} and {morbid-mask} or call a teammate with you to tank Roshan early on to secure it.

Itemize against kiting as you don't necessarily want to switch targets too often due to Fury Swipes stacks.

Consider buying {aghanims-shard} after your first 3 core items to instantly apply Fury Swipes stacks onto enemy heroes and creeps with Earthshock for a massive boost of damage for your follow up right clicks.

You can solo kill tormentor with Enrage to secure the {aghanims-shard}.

## Counter Strategy

Keep distance from Ursa as he is able to build up damage against you with Fury Swipe stacks.

Consider using your disables before or after Ursa's increased status resistance during Enrage.

{euls-scepter-of-divinity} is great for kiting Enrage as it doesn't get affected by its status resistance.

{ghost-scepter}, {force-staff}, {euls-scepter-of-divinity} and {hurricane-pike} are great for kiting Ursa.

Ursa is able to take Roshan early on. {observer-ward} and check Roshan frequently before the 15 minute mark.

Ursa can solo kill tormentor and grant his team an {aghanims-shard}. Place {observer-ward} near the Tormentor or check it regularly when Ursa is missing to prevent a free pickup
