Gliding along her webs, Broodmother births an **army of spiders** to lead her team's forces toward the enemy base. As prey wanders near, she joins her children and attacks, **slowing foes** with incapacitating bites as she indulges her insatiable hunger.

## Strategy

Broodmother is weak in most matchups until level 6. Focus on last hitting and sustaining health until you have Spawn Spiderlings.

Make sure not to feed spiderlings away as that greatly affects your farming speed and kill threat.

Once you have built up an army of spiderlings, try to play on the opponents side of the map as much as you can.

Place {observer-ward} and {sentry-ward} in the enemy jungle so you feel comfortable playing there.

If you are feeling endangered, let your spiderlings farm while staying hidden nearby in the tree line or a cliff under one of your Webs with the hero.

Broodmother can take Roshan down fairly early. Move spiderlings out when Roshan is about to clap.

Use spiderlings to destroy runes to deny them to the enemy mid laner.

Consider using a few of your spiderlings as scouts around the map.

## Counter Strategy

Consider putting a hero that can deal with spiderlings against her on the lane.

Block off Broodmother's jungle camps with {sentry-ward} as she gets closer to level 6.

If you are looking to gank Broodmother, you need at least 3 heroes. Otherwise, pressure other two lanes.

{crimson-guard} and armor items are good against Broodmother.

Healing and regeneration reducing items are good against Insatiable Hunger.

Accuracy items like {monkey-king-bar} are good against Broodmother's Incapacitating Bite.

Broodmother is good at taking an early Roshan. {observer-ward} around Roshan pit and keep check.

Its a solid strategy to gank Broodmother early before she gets level 6 when she's the weakest. Slow her game down.
