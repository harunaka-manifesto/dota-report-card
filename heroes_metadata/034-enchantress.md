## Enchants neutral creeps to do her bidding

Harmful up close and lethal at a distance, Enchantress **skewers foes** with attacks imbued to become more damaging the further they fly. Whether inflicting **powerful slows** on her enemies or **charming forest creatures** to fight her battles, she is never short of tools to win a fight.

## Strategy

Enchant can take over opponents' summons and illusions.

Make sure the nearby neutral camps are unblocked so you can use Enchant.

Enchantress is tanky in the early to mid game due to Untouchable and Nature's Attendants. Play aggressively.

When not much is happening, you can push out side lanes or scout with Enchanted creeps.

Break effects and healing reduction are problematic but you can counter them with items or playstyle adjustments.

Pick up {aghanims-shard} with at least two points in Impetus to massively improve your damage and wave clear ability.

Look to build your {aghanims-scepter} for some lock down against an enemy hero and burst them down with Impetus right clicks.

Look to max out your Enchant and Nature's Attendants and constantly trade with the enemy heroes to win the lane with better HP sustain.

## Counter Strategy

Avoid laning with summon based heroes against Enchantress or use summons carefully on the lane.

Block off pull camps against Enchantress as she will control a neutral creep.

Enchant applies a basic dispel, so be careful with using buffing spells, items or runes.

If Impetus is flying your way, run towards Enchantress or stop moving to take less damage.

It is hard to kill Enchantress with right clicks against Untouchable. Spells are better.
