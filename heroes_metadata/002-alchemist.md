### Unstable Concoction

DAMAGE TYPE: Physical

PIERCES DEBUFF IMMUNITY: No

PASSIVE: No

DISPELLABLE: Strong Dispels Only

* * *

Alchemist brews up an unstable concoction that he can throw at an enemy hero, to stun and deal damage in an area around the explosion. The longer the concoction brews, the more damage it deals and the longer the stun. Alchemist is faster while charging the concoction. After 5 seconds, the brew reaches its maximum damage and stun time. However, after 5.5 seconds, the concoction will explode on Alchemist himself if not thrown.

* * *

While brewing, this ability is replaced by Unstable Concoction Throw. Alchemist can move and act normally while brewing.

A timer above Alchemist's head will indicate the remaining brew time. This timer is visible to enemies.

You can deny yourself with this skill.

Unstable Concoction cannot be disjointed and once thrown Unstable Concoction stops brewing.

Unstable Concoction explosion damages heroes and creeps.

## Strategy

Collecting bounty runes is very important for Alchemist as he gains additional gold from them. Let your team know when the bounty runes are about to spawn.

Use Unstable Concoction to stun your opponents and use that opportunity to secure last hits on ranged creeps in the early stages of the lane.

Alchemist prefers to farm a lot of smaller creeps than fewer large ones as each creep kill provides him bonus gold. Avoid farming ancients until you can kill them reasonably fast. Ask your team to stack smaller camps.

Chemical Rage applies dispel on cast, same as Berserker's Potion. You can use them to dispel {spirit-vessel} or other dispellable debuffs, spells and items.

While farming, look to maintain your Greevil's Greed stacks and try not to stick too long in lanes to wait for creeps or hit buildings.

{blink-dagger} or {shadow-blade} allow you to cast fully channeled Unstable Concoction more easily.

Consider giving {aghanims-scepter} upgrades to your teammates. Depending on their usefulness, you can start giving them out right after your {radiance} purchase. Granting Scepters gives you damage and accelerates your farm.

Alchemist tends to fall off as the game goes. Look to close the game by the 40 minute mark.

## Counter Strategy

{spirit-vessel} and {orb-of-corrosion} are great items for mitigating Alchemist's increadible sustain in the early game.

Block neutral camps with {sentry-ward} so Alchemist can't farm or stack them.

Don't forget to gank Alchemist before he unlocks his ultimate.

Alchemist gets additional gold from Bounty runes. Make sure he does not get any.

Contest Alchemist while he is jungling. {smoke-of-deceit} on him, establish vision in his jungle with {observer-ward}, and block camps with {sentry-ward}.

Build {eye-of-skadi} and {crellas-crozier} to counter Alchemist's HP regen and tanky nature

Alchemist can give {aghanims-scepter} to his teammates. Keep an eye on his inventory to check if he is getting one, and check his teammates afterwards to see if they have the Aghanim's Scepter buff alongside their spells.
