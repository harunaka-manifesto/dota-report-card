## Reflects damage back to his attackers

## Strategy

Focus on last hitting and farm your {bracer}.

If you cannot pressure on the lane or being pressured by enemy heroes, consider creep skipping or pulling enemy creeps to your tower after minute 5 as well as farming neutral camps nearby.

Retaliate works on buildings; so consider tanking tower shots if it is safe enough and if you are tanky enough.

Look around the map to help out your teammates to either escape or chase an enemy down with Stampede.

Once you get {blink-dagger}, try to make use of it immediately before the opponents can see it in your inventory.

Make sure your teammates can follow up on your initiation. Centaur doesn't do insane amounts of damage.

After you purchase {blink-dagger}, make sure you play around an ally that you enable to kill other heroes. These are heroes who do overwhelming damage as a follow up to your stun, such as {skywrath-mage}.

You can try playing behind your allies while they are staying in the frontline and try to bail them from trouble with your {aghanims-scepter}.

You can buy 2 {ring-of-protection} if you are facing heroes that do purely physical damage, such as {phantom-assassin}, {templar-assassin}, {weaver} etc.

## Counter Strategy

Someone should buy {spirit-vessel} against Centaur to deal damage, offset his regeneration and cancel {blink-dagger}.

Armor reducing items are great against Centaur.

Items that offset magical damage output from Centaur are good against him.

Keep checking Centaur's items. Be aware of his {blink-dagger} timing.

Don't clump up in teamfights for Centaur's Blink into Hoof Stomp initiation.
