## Charges down enemies from anywhere on the map

**Highly mobile** and **always aggressive**, Spirit Breaker charges across the map to bring the fight to his enemies. With each bash of his heavy lantern, he knocks his foes senseless. His enemies may run, but with the help of a few wards, they cannot hide.

## Strategy

In order to pressure opponents on the lane, bring creep equilibrium closer to your tower. You should consider dragging creeps from behind the enemy tower or pulling to achieve this.

Consider Charging the furthest target in a line to bash all the opponents heroes on the way.

Movement speed items are amazing for Spirit Breaker as they amplify the Greater Bash damage.

Pop Bulldoze and other movement speed increasing items like {phase-boots} or {shadow-blade} before Charge lands.

Use Charge of Darkness to provide vision over an opponent so he can't juke your allies.

Charge of Darkness allows you to focus on the backliners and supports. Focus on killing them in the fights.

Take your time to farm {shadow-blade} as it provides even more burst and almost guarantees Charge completion.

When not much is happening, push out the side lanes by Charging through all the creeps.

{aghanims-scepter} is a must in the mid game as it lets you do even more damage, Bash more units, and cause a ton of chaos in fights.

## Counter Strategy

Charge of Darkness provides vision over you. Hiding in trees won't help once you're Charged.

Avoid wasting disables on Spirit Breaker buffed by Bulldoze.

Spirit Breaker roams a lot. Keep track of his movements and have a {town-portal-scroll} ready.

Spells and items that provide instant disable are great at stopping Charge of Darkness.

Linken's Sphere is great against Charge of Darkness.
