## Strategy

Focus on last hitting with your high base damage advantage over enemy heroes. Shifting more agility will help with last hitting but can be risky because of your low HP pool.

Be careful when playing versus ranged heroes. You don't want to allow them to harass you too much and force you to morph strength.

Rush {morbid-mask} for HP sustain on a tough lane.

Whenever you drop low on health, shift to agility before using health consumables or {magic-stick}.

Consider putting a point in Adaptive Strike on level 2 if you have issues securing range creep last hit.

Start shifting into strength right away if you expect to be jumped.

Healing reductions and silences are a big problem for your hero, and early {manta-style} can dispel both in many cases.

Don't be morphed fully into agility when moving around or before the fight.

{aghanims-shard} is really good when you are playing against excessive disables and long stuns.

{black-king-bar} is necessary to prevent getting interrupted when Waveforming in to fight enemy heroes.

## Counter Strategy

Someone should buy {spirit-vessel} against Morphling to offset Attribute Shift strength gain.

Morphling is weaker in his earlier levels. Pressure him early on.

Morphling tends to play on a low HP pool. Surprise him with {smoke-of-deceit}, instant disable, silence or invisibility.

Focus Morphling in the fights or he will do insane amounts of damage.

Morphling is likely to be farming safe areas of the map like the triangle. If possible, try to gank with {smoke-of-deceit} and pressure the hero.

{eye-of-skadi} is good against Morphling to offset Attribute Shift (strength gain) and to slow him.
