## Strategy

Use God's Rebuke to secure ranged creep last hits and harass the opponents at the same time.

You often want to set up a Spear of Mars by God's Rebuking first.

Arena of Blood destroys trees in huge AoE, which can be useful against certain heroes.

Dont rush Spearing an enemy during Arena of Blood if they have no ability to get out. It's often times better to Rebuke and Spear with delay.

You can protect your allies from ranged attacks by placing Arena of Blood or using Bulwark.

Don't over-rotate. Focus on getting {blink-dagger} or {euls-scepter-of-divinity} timely.

Make use of the {blink-dagger} immediately before opponents are able to scout it.

Pick up {aghanims-shard} after {blink-dagger} to have even more damage and control with Spear of Mars.

Attack modifying and proc items work with God's Rebuke. You can pick up {satanic} in the late game, for example, to lifesteal back up with the spell.

If you use {blink-dagger} to jump directly on top of an enemy, you can cast Spear of Mars and then quickly drop Arena of Blood while the spear is mid-air, pinning the target to the Arena wall. Works well to lock down elusive enemy heroes.

## Counter Strategy

Avoid playing close to trees against Spear of Mars. Cut some with {quelling-blade} and {tango}.

Mars takes reduced damage from front and sides once he levels up Bulwark. Hit him from the back if possible.

No ranged attacks can hit inside or outside of the walls of Arena of Blood.

Arena of Blood destroys all the trees around it.

Be aware of Mars's {blink-dagger} timing. Look to cancel it in the fights.

Avoid fighting in choke spots and clumping up against {blink-dagger} into Arena of Blood combo.

{black-king-bar} allows you to move in and out of Arena of Blood.

Break effects remove Bulwark and Mars becomes significantly weaker.
