## Strategy

On early levels, make sure Tidebringer hits the opponents while you last hit creeps with your right clicks as that is your main harass tool in the laning stage.

Ideally, you want to cleave off of a creep with Tidebringer for maximum damage.

You can do X Marks the Spot into {town-portal-scroll} to fountain to replenish yourself or allies.

Ask your allies to stack for you as you can easily clear those with level 4 Tidebringer.

Torrent provides vision so you can scout pillars for {observer-ward} or Roshan pit.

You can stack some of the neutral camps by using Torrent two seconds before the minute mark hits.

Make sure to activate {armlet-of-mordiggian} if you have one before using Tidebringer.

Maximum value combo: X Mark -> Ghostship -> activate X Mark close to Ghostship impact -> Torrent after Ghostship lands into Tidebringer.

In fights, if possible align Ghostship in such way that it goes over most of your allies for the Admiral's Rum buff.

In fights, position yourself to cleave with Tidebringer on as many enemy heroes as possible.

X Mark yourself and run forward to kill a dangerous wave with Tidebringer if opponents don't have {euls-scepter-of-divinity}. {blink-dagger} enables you to make this play from even further back.

## Counter Strategy

Kunkka's sword will be glowing when Tidebringer is ready. Play on the side not to get cleaved.

Kunkka will raise his sword when casting Torrent. Look to make a sharp turn.

Kunkka's Ghostship allows his allied heroes that were underneath it to delay 36% damage taken for 10 seconds.

Avoid fighting in choke spots and clumping up against Torrent and Ghostship.

{euls-scepter-of-divinity} is great at stopping X Marks the Spot pullback.
