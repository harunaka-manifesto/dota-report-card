## Strategy

Your base damage and Uproar passive and active effects allow you to win the last hit battle against opponents in the laning stage.

Use Onslaught when opponents are somewhat stationary, for example when they are going for a last hit.

You want to use Uproar when Trampling over the opponents heroes for maximum damage.

Take your time and farm {blink-dagger} so you can instantly Pulverize an opponent.

Try to Pulverize an enemy hero standing in the middle of the enemy lineup so you can stun the rest as well.

When not much is happening, push out side lanes with your AoE damage abilities.

Pick up {aghanims-shard} later in the game as it provides another disable from long range.

Use Trample right as you exit the Onslaught channel to use the charging movement for Trample damage.

Against a tough lane, you can Trample and kill the first wave behind the enemy tier 2 tower.

## Counter Strategy

Avoid staying still against Primal Beast. Move around and be ready to dodge the Onslaught.

Stun, fear, hex and silence effects can stop Pulverize.

Debuff immunity counters all of Primal Beast's abilities other than Uproar.

You can always interrupt Pulverize before completion if you are in range with a stun or silence.
