## Strategy

Before the game starts, make sure to share your units with your team so they can use your Psionic Traps.

Consider skilling Meld on level 1 against melee match-ups.

Keep repositioning to Psi Blade the opponent when last hitting.

You can push the lane in faster with Psi Blades and Refraction in order to secure river runes.

Try to get your level 6 as soon as possible and force your opponent out of the lane.

Ask your teammates to stack for you, especially ancients as you can clear them early.

Psionic Traps provide vision - so place them on rune spots, Roshan pit, and other strategic spots.

You can stack with Psionic Traps but you need to be within the aggro range of neutral creeps.

Don't rotate unnecessarily. Focus on hitting your item timings and pressure opponents' tower.

Focus on bursting squishy backliners and supports, especially with {blink-dagger}.

Templar Assassin is great at taking Roshan early, Look for opportunities to Roshan once you have {desolator}.

You can remain stationary in one spot to gain HP and mana regen while waiting for a creep wave or jungle camps to respawn. Particularly useful to stay healthy in lane.

## Counter Strategy

Damage over time spells and items are great at removing Templar Assassin's Refraction charges.

To dodge Templar Assassin's Psi Blade, play on your hero's max range and avoid being in line with her attack direction.

Templar Assassin struggles against well coordinated ganks in the early game before her Psionic Traps are online.

Make sure to have {sentry-ward} on the lane against Templar Assassin's Psionic Trap.

Templar Assassin tends to stack and farm ancients early on. Block the camp with {sentry-ward} or contest her.

Carry detection on multiple heroes against Templar Assassin.

Templar Assassin takes down buildings fast with {desolator}. Organize defense quickly.

Armor items, {ghost-scepter} and {heavens-halberd} are great against Templar Assassin.

Be aware of Templar Assassin's {desolator} and {blink-dagger} timing. She can solo kill most heroes.

Templar Assassin is able to take Roshan early on. {observer-ward} and check Roshan.

Check the Roshan pit for Psionic Trap before taking Roshan.
