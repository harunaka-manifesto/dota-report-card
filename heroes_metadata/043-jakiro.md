## Freezes and burns enemies with magic

Jakiro is their own best friend. Not content to merely punish enemies with **fire**, the twins **freeze them in place** to force them to stand there and burn. With foes aflame, they turn to **inflicting massive damage** on enemy structures with their liquid fire.

## Strategy

Make sure camps are open for pulling as the lane will likely push due to AoE damage of your spells.

Jakiro is moderately tanky, so look to soak up damage for your core in the lane. But be aware of your positioning while doing so as your hero is slow.

Ice Path provides vision so you can use it to scout pillars for {observer-ward} and Roshan pit.

Jakiro is great at defending towers due to Macropyre and your cores can split push in meantime.

Use Liquid Fire to apply pressure on enemy buildings.

In teamfight, take your time to land the most impactful Ice Paths and Macropyre.

When not much is happening on the map, you can split push easily with your spells without even showing yourself.

Pick up {aghanims-shard} after your first couple of core items to remove the mana cost and shared cooldown of Liquid Fire and Liquid Frost and throw them both with your double attack right clicks.

You can carry late game fights by nicely positioning Macropyre improved by {aghanims-scepter}.

## Counter Strategy

Cast point on Jakiro's spells are long, so you can possibly dodge them.

Avoid standing beside your laning partner against Dual Breath and Ice Path.

Jakiro is great at destroying towers with Liquid Fire. Organize defense quickly.

Jakiro is great at defending towers alone. Jump him or consider glyphing the wave.

Avoid fighting in choke spots and clumping up against Ice Path and Macropyre combo.

Magic resistance items and debuff immunity are great against Jakiro.
