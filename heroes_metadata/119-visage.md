As his **deadly familiars** scout the field, Visage **cloaks himself in armor** and seeks the next battle. Once near a skirmish, he **gathers soul energy** from damage inflicted upon nearby fighters before releasing it in a devastating attack.

### Gravekeeper's Cloak

ABILITY: Passive

PASSIVE: Breakable

* * *

Visage generates a layered barrier that protects him from damage. If he receives damage from a player, one layer is removed, and takes time to recover.

Visage's Familiars are protected by his cloak when within 900 range of him, based on the number of layers Visage has.

* * *

Each time Visage receives damage greater than the Minimum Damage that was not self-inflicted and was from a player owned source, a layer is removed.

Familiars within **900** range of Visage will receive the current damage reduction from Gravekeeper's Cloak.

Break disables the gaining of new stacks.

Break does not disable the bonuses from existing stacks.

## Strategy

Visage is fairly weak until level 6. Try to kill him before he gets stronger with his Familiars.

Familiars are fairly squishy when Visage is not around.

Use Familiars to control runes, stack camps, gank lanes even without your hero, as well as drag and cut creep waves.

You can summon fresh Familiars to be able to chain 4 stuns on opponents.

With {aghanims-scepter}, your mobility and pick off potential increases even more with invisibility and other benefits on Silent as the Grave.

Pick up {aghanims-shard} later in the game as it allows you to survive jumps, buy time, and provides another stun.

With {boots-of-travel}, you can {town-portal-scroll} around the map to join fight or split push and bring your Familiars to you with the alternate cast of Summon Familiars.

## Counter Strategy

Instances of 40 damage and higher will remove a layer of Gravekeeper's Cloak.

Visage's Familiars give a lot of gold and are easy to kill when Visage is not around.

Visage is a snowbally hero and falls off in late game. Look to extend the game. Don't be greedy with items.

Visage takes down buildings fast with Familiars. Organize defense quickly.

Break effects remove Gravekeeper's Cloak and Visage and his nearby Familiars becomes much weaker.

{crimson-guard} is great against the physical damage of Visage and his Familiars.

Once Visage gets {aghanims-scepter}, place {observer-ward} and {sentry-ward} together to track his movement and carry detection.
