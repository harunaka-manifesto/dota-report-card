## Strategy

Consider Fissure blocking the first wave to have better creep equilibrium.

Use Enchant Totem to harass opponents, secure creep last hits, especially ranged ones, and to deny friendly creeps.

At the end of the laning stage, find a lane for yourself to farm a {blink-dagger} and catch up with levels.

Once you get {blink-dagger}, try to make use of it immediately before your opponents see it in your inventory.

Avoid showing yourself so the opponents forget about you and clump up, and your {blink-dagger} doesn't get canceled.

In fights, split the opponents with Fissure into two parts or isolate an opponent from the rest of their team.

## Counter Strategy

If Earthshaker is blocking the first wave with fissure, look to pull back the lane as soon as possible

Earthshaker uses fissure to block off heroes and land kills. Be mindful of your positioning

Keep checking Earthshaker to see if he has {blink-dagger} yet. Don't let him surprise you with multi-hero Echo Slam

Once Earthshaker has {blink-dagger} or {aghanims-scepter}, control him in teamfights before he uses all his spells

Earthshaker is likely to have {blink-dagger} by now

Don't clump up or Earthshaker might kill you all

Earthshaker might also buy {shadow-blade}. If he does, purchase detection
