## Can freeze his enemies in time

Time is everything to Faceless Void. He speeds time to **dash between locations**, manipulates it to **dodge attacks**, stops it in a large area to devastate his foes, and given enough of it to gather resources, he can make himself unbelievably powerful.

## Strategy

Be careful about using Time Walk aggressively on early levels as you leave yourself exposed for a while. Only use it when you have taken substantial damage in the last 2 seconds.

Look around the map and be ready to {town-portal-scroll} in and pick-off an opponent but only when you have Chronosphere ready. This contributes to putting pressure around the map.

Faceless Void is an attack speed hungry hero as damage per second is closely related to the frequency of Time Lock procs. That is why {mask-of-madness} and {mjollnir} are often your must have items.

In teamfights, look to trap as many opponents as possible in Chronosphere while leaving allies outside. Avoid including your important ally heroes inside the Chronosphere.

Avoid playing aggressively when Chronosphere is on cooldown and push out lanes instead.

You can tank up and get mobility items if your teammates can provide plenty of damage in Chronosphere.

{mask-of-madness} enables you to pick off free kills easily with Chronosphere. Look for these opening kills around the map whenever your ultimate is up with Mask of Madness.

If you bought {battle-fury}, look to farm quickly towards your {aghanims-scepter} so you can constantly go in and out of fights with Time Walk to do damage and control enemy heroes for your team.

Get {aghanims-shard} after your first few core items to be a nuisance in fights and build up your attack speed and movement speed with Time Dilation on enemy heroes.

## Counter Strategy

Cause damage to Faceless Void over time, so he can't heal back all of it from Time Walk.

Maximize damage on Faceless Void after he has used Time Walk.

Faceless Void relies on Chronosphere to be effective and he will feel underwhelming if it is down. Make use of the long cooldown of his ultimate to force a fight or take an objective.

Don't clump up when Chronosphere is up. Save the hero that has been caught or look to interrupt the follow-up damage from Faceless Void's teammates

Time Dilation can be a nuisance to all heroes - so having basic dispels are recommended.

Items like {aeon-disk}, {ethereal-blade} and Aegis are good counters against Chronosphere in the mid to late game. It is advised to keep buyback ready as you might want to use your first life to burn through Faceless Void's ultimate.

You can try to bait out Chronosphere with smart illusion rune usage or {manta-style}.
