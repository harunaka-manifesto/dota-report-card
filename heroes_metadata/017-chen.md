## Converts creeps to his cause, building an army

Converting the creatures of the forest to his cause, Chen leads his **makeshift armies** to aid his team in battle. He assaults enemies with beasts while sending friends to safety. Then, when things seem darkest, he **heals his allies** wherever they may be.

## Strategy

Bring a {sentry-ward} or two to the lane to unblock the camps. Chen's laning impact is directly related to summon usage.

If you find Harpy Stormcrafter, you will have massive kill potential with the slow from Penitence.

Aside from using summons to harass on the lane, you can gank with them, stack camps, control runes, block camps and even snipe couriers.

Keep an eye on the map and save your teammates in trouble by using Hand of God.

You want to group up early with your team and make use of your summons and auras to play aggressively.

When not much is happening on the map, you can split push lanes with your summons.

Look to farm and play greedy once you have level 3 Holy Persuasion. An early timing on {mekansm} into {solar-crest} lets you snowball the game.

You can unsummon a creep with Holy Persuasion even on the other side of the map once they have done their split-push job to prevent the enemy team from farming them and also not have them take up space in your army.

## Counter Strategy

Block off small and big pull camps either with a {sentry-ward} or with your hero's body.

Chen lineups tend to power spike in early to mid game. Don't buy greedy items.

Look to gank Chen as you will kill his army as well.

Items that reduce healing are good against Chen.

Chen lineups are good at taking early Roshan. Place {observer-ward} around the Roshan pit to scout.

Be aware of Chen applying strong dispel with Hand of God if he has the level 25 talent.

Once Chen has {aghanims-scepter}, you will have to stun or silence him to prevent him from channeling his ultimate and disable the BKB effect on his surrounding allies
