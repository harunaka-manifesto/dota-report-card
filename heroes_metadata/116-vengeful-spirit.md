## Can swap herself with any nearby hero

Vengeful Spirit's presence is felt at all times, whether she is alive or dead. Able to **swap positions** with any nearby hero, **stun enemies**, and reduce her killer's effectiveness if she falls, she constantly brings confusion and chaos to her foes.

## Strategy

Use Wave of Terror to secure ranged creeps and harass the opponents at the same time.

You can stack multiple camps at the same time and help clear them with Wave of Terror.

Wave of Terror provides vision so you can scout pillars for {observer-and-sentry-wards} and Roshan pit.

The most important thing in the fights is to be ready to Swap an ally in trouble or an opponent out of position.

Vengeful Spirit enables an early Roshan kill due to Wave of Terror, Vengeance Aura and possibly minus armor items.

Pick up {aghanims-shard} once you have {aghanims-scepter} to stun multiple heroes in team fights.

In fights, you can Swap an ally just to give them and yourself a damage barrier, as long as it does not put them in a bad position.

## Counter Strategy

Focus Vengeful Spirit in the fights as she provides saves and empowers her cores.

Armor items are great against Vengeful Spirit's Wave of Terror and Vengeance Aura.

{linkens-sphere} and a well timed {lotus-orb} are great against Magic Missile and Nether Swap.

If Vengeful bought {aghanims-scepter}, then you should ignore her in fights. If you focus her at the start, you will also have to kill her illusion afterwards.
