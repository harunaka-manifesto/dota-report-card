## Strategy

Harass with Burning Spears as often as you can as it doesn't pull aggro. Your health loss will recover over time with Berserker's Blood.

On tough lanes, rush {helm-of-iron-will} to solve health sustain issues.

Huskar is a snowball type hero who applies consistent pressure right from the start. Do well in the lane, take an early Roshan and call for your team to group up around you.

Lifebreak dispels on cast, which is particularly useful against heal reductions like {spirit-vessel}.

Do Roshan once you have {armlet-of-mordiggian} completed and ideally Berserker's Blood and Burning Spears maxed out.

Check opponents for heal reductions and break effects. You must itemize or adjust playstyle accordingly.

{aghanims-scepter} allows you to pick-off heroes and deals with kiting.

Get shard later in the game to have another dispel on Berserker's Blood along with Life Break to make it difficult for the enemy team to kite you or maintain {spirit-vessel} on you.

## Counter Strategy

Someone should buy {spirit-vessel} against Huskar to offset his regeneration and deal high damage.

Huskar likes to play on low HP. Look to gank him early on.

Life Break applies basic dispel. Avoid casting dispellable spells and items before Huskar jumps.

Huskar is a snowball type hero who relies on killing Roshan early on. Place {observer-ward} and check the Roshan pit.

{heavens-halberd} disarms Huskar for 3.5 seconds.

{silver-edge} breaks Huskar's passive, Berserker's Blood, making him significantly less tanky.
