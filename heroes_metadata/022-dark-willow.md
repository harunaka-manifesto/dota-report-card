## Strategy

Consider using Shadow Realm to secure range creep last hits.

You can dodge ranged attacks and many spell projectiles with Shadow Realm.

If in trouble, you can potentially do Shadow Realm into {town-portal-scroll} out play.

Look to pick off isolated enemy heroes with no escape or dispel with your lockdown into the insane Bedlam damage.

In teamfights, focus on getting off Terrorize, Bramble Maze and Cursed Crown timely and then burst an isolated target with Bedlam.

If the game opens up for you, you can transition into right clicker with {aghanims-scepter} and attack speed items in the late game.

Look to steal the enemy Wisdom shrine. Your lockdown lets you keep an enemy hero out of the shrine area if they are late, and you can also Shadow Realm and {town-portal-scroll} out against most heroes if your play doesn't work out.

You have increased HP and mana regen in the Shadow Realm duration. You can use this to have more efficiency with {tango}, {healing-salve}, or {tranquil-boots} regen when low on health.

## Counter Strategy

Dark Willow is squishy at the start and doesn't trade well. But be careful about running into Brambles when engaging with her.

Under the effect of Cursed Crown, make sure not to get too close to your allies or they will be stunned too.

Bedlam does a lot of damage to a single target. Share the damage with allied units.

Items that provide dispels are generally good against Cursed Crown, Bramble Maze and Terrorize.

{black-king-bar} is great against Dark Willow's disables and magical damage output.

Don't clump up in teamfights because of Terrorize and Cursed Crown.
