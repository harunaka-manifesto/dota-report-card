## Able to force her enemies to attack their own ally

## Strategy

Make sure to hit each enemy heroes at least once to apply the Arctic Burn debuff. Attacking the same enemy hero multiple times also reapplies the debuff with each right click.

Avoid targeting melee creeps with Splinter Blast until it reaches level 4 as you will put the ranged creep in deny range.

Be careful when using Cold Embrace as a save. Often, it is better to use your slows or Winter's Curse.

It is good to play close to trees or cliffs in dangerous spots on the map, so you can use them to escape with Arctic Burn.

Avoid showing yourself early in fights. Stay hidden and play around your Winter's Curse and Cold Embrace. Use Arctic Burn to reposition.

Use Winter's Curse carefully in the middle of fights as you might waste a lot of your ally's damage and cooldowns.

{aghanims-scepter} is crucial for right-click build. Make sure you take time to farm it.

Having timely {blink-dagger} will allow you to seize the moment and land a multi-hero Winter's Curse.

Fight around good vision so you can position well and land a multi-hero Winter's Curse.

Consider split pushing with Splinter Blast from the tree line. It is going to be your intuitive decision whether to right click the wave or just blast it. Use Arctic Burn across trees and hills to get away from danger.

Use Eldwurm's Edda to boost the level of one of your abilities based on whether you need more damage or save potential in the game, but wait till that ability is maxed out. Your priority should be: Arctic Burn > Splinter Blast > Cold Embrace

## Counter Strategy

Winter Wyvern is bad at trading when Arctic Burn is down.

Cold Embrace prevents all physical damage.

Focus Winter Wyvern in fights as she provides saves and disables for her team. Try to find her.

Avoid clumping up and fighting in choke spots against Winter's Curse.

{black-king-bar} prevents you from hitting a Winter's Cursed unit, but you can still get Cursed yourself.

Status resistance items shorten the duration of Winter's Curse.
