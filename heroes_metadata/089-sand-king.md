## Strategy

Make sure pull camps are open for pulling as you will be pushing the lane non-stop.

Check opponents' inventories for detection frequently.

Tag creeps with Caustic Finale and if an opponent comes close, you can Burrowstrike or Stinger to last hit and explode creeps.

Sand King is great at defending towers as you can Sand Storm in front and hide yourself in trees.

Once you have {blink-dagger}, try to surprise opponents before they are able to see it in your inventory.

Stay out of vision and be patient in the fights. Choose a good moment to jump with your combo. Highly advisable you cast Epicenter before going in with your {blink-dagger} and Burrowstrike.

Sand King is great at split pushing when not much is happening on the map.

After you buy your {blink-dagger}, make sure you play alongside an allied hero who can provide damage to follow up on your Burrowstrike initiation.

## Counter Strategy

Bring a {sentry-ward} to the lane against Sand King's Sand Storm.

Your creeps explode on death due to Sand King's Caustic Finale. Denying affected creeps prevents explosion.

Avoid fighting in Sand Storm for too long as it does a lot of magical damage.

Against Sand King, you need to carry detection on multiple heroes.

Be aware of Sand King's {blink-dagger} timing. Look to cancel his Dagger in fights.

Avoid fighting in choke spots and clumping up against Sand King's Epicenter and {blink-dagger} combination.

Debuff immunity and magic resistance items are great against Sand King's magical damage.
