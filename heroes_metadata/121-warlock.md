## Strategy

Prioritize using Shadow Word for healing instead for damage due to magic resistance. You can heal yourself and your laning partner at the same time in the 225 AoE of Shadow Word.

Fatal Bonds does not latch onto fogged units. However, if you latch Fatal Bonds onto a target, they will still take damage even if they run into fog.

Use Fatal Bonds to harass if you can link four lane creeps and multiple enemy heroes on the lane.

Chaotic Offering destroys trees upon landing.

Be active when Chaotic Offering is up, organize {smoke-of-deceit} and farm with golem after the fight.

Pick up {aghanims-shard} around minute 15 as it improves your wave clear and damage significantly.

Once you have {aghanims-shard}, you can use Upheavel to farm creep waves and jungle camps from a distance.

Make sure to keep the pull camps unblocked to bring back the lane creeps closer to your tower as Fatal Bonds will constantly push them out.

## Counter Strategy

Warlock's Chaotic Offering has long cooldown. Look to fight when it is down.

Avoid clumping up and fighting in choke spots against Warlock's spells.

Most of Warlock's damage comes from Fatal Bonds. Dispel items are great at removing them.

Debuff immunity is great against Warlock's spells. However, Chaotic Offering will still stun you through {black-king-bar}

Be aware of Warlock's {aghanims-scepter} and {refresher-orb} timings. He can carry the game.
