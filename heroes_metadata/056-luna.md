## Strategy

Use Lucent Beam to secure ranged creep last hits if necessary.

Lunar Blessing allows you and your ally to trade stronger and last hit better against the enemy heroes.

Generally, two points in Lucent Beam is enough for you to secure last hits in lane and win trades with a decent nuke spell. However, you can proceed on maxing Lucent Beam if you are dominating the lane and want to be aggressive with Eclipse.

Don't forget to use Lunar Orbit in lane when trading to dish out extra damage and tank lesser incoming damage.

Target creeps with higher health as the bounces of Moon Glaives does lesser damage with each bounce.

Alert your teammates to stack for you as you can clear stacks easily with Moon Glaives.

Prioritize farming ancient camps once you have {mask-of-madness} instead of normal camps unless given free lane and tower pressure.

Make use of {manta-style} illusions to push out lanes and farm extra creep waves that you are not able to with your hero.

Activate Lunar Orbit when farming ancient creeps for lesser incoming damage and higher damage output.

Try to eliminate squishy heroes by jumping on top of them with {blink-dagger} and bursting them.

## Counter Strategy

Luna has short attack range and is susceptible to early harass. Make sure to pressure her every time she is left alone by her support in lane.

Be mindful about trading with Luna when her support is present as they have high right click damage from Lunar Blessing aura.

Luna's Eclipse does a lot of damage. Keep distance from Luna or share damage with allied units.

Luna farms quickly due to Moon Glaives. Block camps with {sentry-ward}, place deep {observer-ward} and gank her with {smoke-of-deceit}.

Avoid clumping up in teamfights as Moon Glaives will bounce between you and your allies.
