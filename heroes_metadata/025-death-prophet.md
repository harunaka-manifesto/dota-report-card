## Strategy

Secure ranged creep last hits with Crypt Swarm and possibly hit opponents at the same time.

Try to throw an auto attack and use Crypt Swarm at the same time to secure last hits.

Death Prophet's survivability is related to Spirit Siphon usage. Siphon as many units as possible for a decent duration. You should be looking to dive towers with Siphon charges from level 5, especially 6 onwards with Exorcism.

Death Prophet excels at taking towers and Roshan early with Exorcism. Call for your team to group up around you. Play more aggressively and in the enemy territory when Exorcism is available.

Avoid playing aggressively when Exorcism is down. Push out waves and prepare for the next move.

Itemize for survivability and to stay in the middle of the fight. Exorcism and Spirit Siphon will do the damage.

When in trouble, you can Spirit Siphon opponents and use {euls-scepter-of-divinity} on yourself. You will still be healed in the air.

Healing reductions, such as {spirit-vessel}, are a great problem for Death Prophet. You must itemize against some of them.

## Counter Strategy

When Death Prophet has Exorcism running, either burst her down or run away.

Look to defend towers against Death Prophet and fight her when Exorcism ends.

Items that reduce healing, such as {spirit-vessel}, are good against Death Prophet's Spirit Siphons and Exorcism.

Armor items are good against the physical damage of Exorcism.

Items that provide dispels are good against Death Prophet's Silence.
