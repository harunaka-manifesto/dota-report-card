## Traps and silences foes in his static storm

Disruptor excels at shattering the plans of his enemies. **Summoning impassable fences** to trap targets as he calls down a **silencing storm**, should a foe attempt something unexpected he simply **teleports them back** to their earlier location.

## Strategy

Make use of Thunder Strike's movement speed slow and vision over the enemy hero to follow up with a few right clicks.

Whenever you see or expect an opponent to {town-portal-scroll} in, be ready to Glimpse him back.

Kinetic Field provides vision in the middle of it. You can use it to scout pillars for {observer-ward} or Roshan pit.

Glimpse instantly destroys basic illusions.

Pick up {aghanims-shard} later in the game to have another tool to block and trap enemies with Kinetic Fence.

{blink-dagger} is a perfect item to set up your Static Storm. When combined with {aghanims-scepter} and {refresher-orb}, you can single-handedly turn or win teamfights.

## Counter Strategy

Disruptor's main harassing tool is Thunder Strike. Heroes with dispel abilities are strong against him

You can use Disruptor's Thunder Strike AoE damage by walking into your creep wave and damaging it. This allows you to manipulate the wave equilibrium and potentially secure more denies.

Don't {town-portal-scroll} in plain sight around {disruptor} as you might get Glimpsed back to where you came from

Hide behind trees or run to highground before using {town-portal-scroll} or escaping, so you don't get caught by Glimpse.

Disruptor's Glimpse can be dodged by certain spells and items such as {black-king-bar} or {manta-style}

All of Disruptor's spells are countered by {black-king-bar} until he has {aghanims-scepter}. Once he has it, you won't be able to activate {black-king-bar} under Static Storm as all items are muted

Don't clump up because of Disruptor's Kinetic Field and Static Storm combination. It takes 1 second for Kinetic Field to form so you have a small window to get out. You can't {force-staff} out of it and many other mobility spells are not working
