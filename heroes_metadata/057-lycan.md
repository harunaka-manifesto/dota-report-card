## Strategy

Rush {helm-of-iron-will} to solve your health sustain issues.

You can scout enemy whereabouts with Wolves before the 0 minute runes and check for the mid lane {observer-ward}.

Focus on securing last hits and denying as you have extra damage from the Wolves and Feral Impulse.

Avoid feeding summons to opponents. Deny them or send them away, perhaps to block a camp or control a rune.

Lycan's laning power spike is {helm-of-the-dominator} and level 6. Try to get a kill or force the enemy out of the lane and you can take the tower fast.

Delay death of the 5 minute catapult wave to meet {helm-of-the-dominator} and level 6 power spike so you can take the enemy tower.

Take control of a neutral creep with a stun or root with the {helm-of-the-dominator} so the opponents can't {town-portal-scroll} out when you chase them during your ultimate.

When your dominated creep is about to die, if it has not taken damage recently, you can take over another one and deny gold and experience to opponents.

When not much is happening, push out the side lanes with summons. Black Dragon is particularly good for that.

Lycan is great at killing Roshan due to summons, Howl and auras. Bring him down by the 20 minute mark.

Consider using Wolves to scout for enemy heroes in the mid to late game. You can move-click them on an enemy hero to follow them around. You can deny runes as well.

{aghanims-scepter}'s Wolf Bite is a huge buff for one of your right-clicking cores.

In tough late game scenarios, resort to split pushing by running your summons down lanes.

Use your burst damage to make maximum use of item spikes by looking for kills and objectives on the map.

Look to isolate and kill enemy supports quickly in fights as they will find it hard to kite you without items.

Try to initiate fights or gather around your teammates when your ultimate is up. If your ultimate is on cooldown, you should be pushing lanes or split pushing and pressuring towers.

Consider using your Wolves to help your laning partner win the trade-offs against the enemy support.

Get {aghanims-shard} once you have {helm-of-the-overlord} to summon an extra Wolf and give them the Hightail ability. Afterwards, you can run your Wolves after an enemy support as they have massive kill threat on their own.

## Counter Strategy

Lycan is a low armor hero. Pressure him with your right clicks early in the game before he gets {helm-of-iron-will}.

Try to kill the Wolves, especially when they are on low levels.

Be aware of Lycan's level 6 and {helm-of-the-dominator} timing.

Lycan and his summons do a lot of damage to buildings. Organize defense quickly or send a hero that can naturally defend towers.

Lycan is a snowball type hero who pressures early and falls off later on. Look to extend the game. Don't be greedy with items.

{crimson-guard} and armor items are great against the physical damage output of Lycan and his summons.

Lycan is great at split pushing and ratting. Consider getting {boots-of-travel} on a core.

Lycan is able to kill Roshan early on. Place {observer-ward} and check the Roshan pit.

Look to gank Lycan when you know his ultimate is down. He is fairly useless without it.
