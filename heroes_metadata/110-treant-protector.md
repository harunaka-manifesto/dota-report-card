## Strategy

Treant Protector has the highest base damage in the game at level 1. So make use of it to harass enemy heroes and deny creeps.

Look for angles to cut off opponents with Nature's Grasp. Make sure to catch a tree in its path to do extra damage with the spell.

Leech Seed continues healing even after the unit it was used on dies.

Look around the map to heal and save allies and buildings with Living Armor.

Treant Protector is great at defending towers safely with Nature's Grasp and Living Armor.

Don't rush with Overgrowth in fights if opponents have multiple ways to dispel it at the start. Wait for them to waste their dispel tools.

Treant Protector is amazing at split pushing with Nature's Guise and Nature's Grasp. Look to push out side lanes and soak XP at dangerous places on the map even if it costs you your life on occasion.

Pick up {aghanims-shard} around minute 15 to place vision around the map with Eyes in the Forest.

{aghanims-scepter} lets you use Overgrowth more than once in drawn-out fights, while also letting you output massive right click damage.

## Counter Strategy

Use your {quelling-blade} non-stop on the trees where you expect Treant Protector to approach from and make his passive ability less useful to operate around the lane effectively.

Consider buying {boots-of-speed} on some supports because in most cases, you cannot trade effectively with Treant Protector due to him having the highest attack damage at level 1.

Take towers down in one go so Treant Protector can't heal them with Living Armor.

Be aware of Treant Protector's {meteor-hammer} timing if he is gearing up towards the item. He becomes good at split pushing.

Treant Protector can become invisible around trees through his innate. Carry detection on multiple heroes.

{observer-and-sentry-wards} at the outer jungles of the map are great at stopping Treant Protector from split pushing.

Items that apply dispel on you are great against Treant Protector's Overgrowth and Leech Seed.

Once Treant Protector gets {aghanims-shard}, consider getting a {gem-of-true-sight} and destroy Eyes in the Forest trees with right clicks or a {quelling-blade}.
