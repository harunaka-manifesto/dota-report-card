## Commands a powerful spirit bear in battle

Battling alongside his **powerful spirit bear**, Lone Druid demolishes enemy forces and structures. Switching as needed between **ranged and melee attacks** with his transformation, should he have enough time to arm and strengthen both himself and his companion, there is little that can stop them.

## Abilities

Summon Spirit Bear

PASSIVE: No

* * *

Summons a powerful Spirit Bear companion that can equip items.

If the bear moves 1100 distance away from the Lone Druid, it cannot attack. Lone Druid suffers 20% of his max health as backlash damage if the Spirit Bear dies. Spirit Bear has Demolish, Return, Entangling Claws, Savage Roar, and Spirit Link abilities.

* * *

Most spells interact with the Spirit Bear in the same way as they do with heroes.

Spirit Bear has 0 base attributes, 0 base armor, and **%base_magic_resistance%** base magic resistance. Attributes from buffs or items fully affect the bear as they would for Universal heroes.

Spirit Bear cannot gain experience. The Spirit Bear levels up when Lone Druid levels up.

Spirit Bear has its own Aghanim's Scepter and Shard upgrades.

Spirit Bear receives a copy of Lone Druid's Neutral Item that has an independent cooldown.

### Entangle

AFFECTS: Area

DAMAGE TYPE: Magical

PIERCES DEBUFF IMMUNITY: Yes

PASSIVE: No

DEBUFF: Yes

* * *

Allows Lone Druid to Entangle enemies once they gain 5 stacks. Entangled enemies are rooted and take damage over time.

When cast, applies 2 stacks to each enemy hero in the area and 5 stacks to enemy creeps. Also empowers Lone Druid for 10s, making him apply 1 stack with each attack on enemy heroes. Enemies cannot gain stacks while Entangled.

Leveling this ability also levels Spirit Bear's Entangling Claws ability.

* * *

Rooted units can't use Blink abilities and are revealed if they are invisible.

Lone Druid's Empowered Buff and enemy stack counter debuff cannot be dispelled.

Entangled debuff is dispellable.

Creeps can't gain stacks from either Lone Druid's or Spirit Bear's attacks, and are Entangled only on cast.

Roshan is affected as if he were a hero.

## Strategy

Control runes with your hero and Spirit Bear.

Poke enemy heroes here and there to build up some Entangle stacks. If you manage to get 3 stacks, you can follow up with Entangle from your hero to root them and follow up with more right clicks.

Make use of catapult waves to take down towers. The Spirit Bear can tank tower shots for a while.

When in trouble, consider using Savage Roar and True Form. Lifesteal or body blocks from the Spirit Bear can also help survive.

Avoid showing your hero too much, especially at the start of the fight.

You can take Roshan fairly early by yourself or with minimal help. Your hero and Spirit Bear can take turns tanking the damage and heal each other with right clicks.

Make sure to pick up {aghanims-shard} on your hero at the 15 minute mark. It provides a buff similar to {drum-of-endurance} and basic dispel with Savage Roar.

In tough late game scenarios, resort to ratting with {aghanims-scepter} on your Spirit Bear to let it attack away from your hero.

## Counter Strategy

Spirit Link heals Lone Druid every time the Bear hits. Burst Lone Druid or don't bother hitting him.

Lone Druid buys items for the Spirit Bear. If you kill Spirit Bear twice, he does no damage.

Spirit Bear does a lot of damage to buildings. Organize defense quickly.

Lone Druid is a snowbally hero and falls off as the game goes. Look to extend the game.

Lone Druid is great at split-pushing and ratting. Consider getting {boots-of-travel} on a core.
