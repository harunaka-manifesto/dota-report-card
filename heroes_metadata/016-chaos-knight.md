## Strategy

Try to get level 2 before your opponent and go for a kill with your support. Chaos Knight has good kill potential with levels. Try to catch enemies that are alone in 2 versus 1 trades.

Try to have Chaos Strike maxed by level 7 as it is your main tool for farming and will help with burst damage early. It also provides you with lifesteal to not have to go back to base while farming.

Use Phantasm off cooldown not just to fight but also to farm faster unless you suspect a fight will happen soon. Prioritize ancient creeps whenever possible with Phantasm usage.

Phantasm illusions scale with stat attributes, attack speed, movement speed increase, health regeneration, and some attack modifiers like lifesteal and critical strike.

Try to target and burst enemy supports and save heroes on the enemy team at the start of fights. Your illusions will generally be killed off as the fight continues.

With {blink-dagger}, you can use Phantasm, Blink in and pull illusions on target by using Reality Rift. Catch the important backline heroes with this like {sniper} and {oracle}.

## Counter Strategy

Chaos Strike sustains Chaos Knight. Deal bursts of damage to him in lane so he can't hit creeps easily and regain HP. Keep him low all the time.

Chaos Knight's Phantasm applies a basic dispel on cast.

Either kill Chaos Knight's illusions in the fights or disengage. Kite him around with slows and disables and keep distance from his Reality Rift.

Items that deal AoE damage and AoE disables are good against Chaos Knight's illusions. Look to buy items like {gleipnir} for AoE disable.

{crimson-guard} and armor items are good at offsetting Chaos Knight's physical damage output.
