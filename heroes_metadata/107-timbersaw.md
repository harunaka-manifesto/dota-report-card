## Strategy

Use Whirling Death to secure last hits, harass opponents and, at the same time, cut some trees to gain mana through your innate.

Consider putting a point in the Timber Chain early when facing a tough lane.

Keep Reactive Armor stacks high to be able to play aggressively and not fear for your life.

Check how many {magic-stick} charges opponents have when you are going for a kill.

Consider tanking tower shots to keep the catapult alive if you have Reactive Armor leveled up.

Timbersaw isn't the best at rotating. You'd rather force opponents to come to you by playing aggressively.

Consider parking yourself in front of the tower opponents are looking to destroy. Build up Reactive Armor stacks though.

Items that reduce healing and break effects are a big problem. Itemize against them and adjust playstyle.

When not much is happening, push out the side lanes with your spells. You can even throw your Chakram from long range for the purpose.

Pick up {aghanims-shard} later in the game as it improves your DPS and provides some much needed building damage.

You do not necessarily want to go to late game, so try to close out the game before you fall off.

## Counter Strategy

Someone should buy {spirit-vessel} against Timbersaw's Reactive Armor

Play agressively against Timbersaw before he reaches level 3, or only when his Reactive Armor stacks are low. His weakest phase is his early levels.

Stay away from trees against Timbersaw

Timbersaw is strongest between 8 and 20 minutes, so avoid contesting him on the lane and try to pressure other lanes and heroes instead

Buy {silver-edge} against Timbersaw's reactive armor, if you need an additional item to deal with him

Items that let you dispel Timbersaw, such as {euls-scepter-of-divinity} or {nullifier}, let you instantly take off his damage barrier when he activates Reactive Armor with {aghanims-scepter}.
