## Unleashes a powerful attack that bounces between enemies

Drifting from battle to battle and **sacrificing creeps** to fuel his assaults, Lich is always ready to help his fellow heroes destroy their enemies with **slowing frost**. Should his foes be foolish enough to gather, his **bouncing ultimate** will ensure their demise.

## Strategy

Make use of the slowing part of the Frost Blast to chain a couple of right clicks.

Secure ranged creep last hit with Frost Blast or a right click when your core can't safely do it.

Play aggressively on the lane, burn your mana by using your spells and utilize the regen items you bought at the start of the game.

At level 6, you can even solo kill opponents if there are creeps for Chain Frost to bounce.

Frost Shield is great for defending towers and delaying the enemy push.

In fights, be quick at saving allies with Frost Shield and defensive items. Use other abilities afterwards.

When not much is happening, push out side lanes with Frost Shield and Frost Blast.

With {aghanims-scepter}, you can still cast all your other spells while channeling Sinister Gaze.

Use Sacrifice to deny one of your lane creeps to gain mana and experience. Look to deny outside the experience range of enemy heroes. Sacrificing the ranged creep provides more experience, while the melee creep would give you more mana.

## Counter Strategy

Lich is a strong laner. Bring extra consumables.

Frost Shield from Lich significantly reduces damage taken from right clicks and it can be used on towers.

Split up when Lich uses Chain Frost or run towards a group of creeps to pass the bounce away.

Avoid fighting in choke spots and clumping up against Chain Frost.

Magic resistance and debuff immunity items are great against Lich.

{lotus-orb} is great at reflecting all of Lich's offensive spells.

Lich will Sacrifice one of his creeps, resulting in the lane pushing towards the enemy tower. Use that against him by pulling or getting aggressive with more creeps on your side.
