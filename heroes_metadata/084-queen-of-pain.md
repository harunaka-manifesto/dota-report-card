## Strategy

Use Scream of Pain to secure ranged creeps and hit opponents at the same time.

Avoid investing too many points in Shadow Strike in the laning stage. It will slow down your farming and make mid game fights tougher with the lack of AoE damage.

When in trouble, Blink into fog, into tree lines or over cliffs.

Look around for opportunities to gank once you have Sonic Wave and control runes.

Blink or position yourself in such a way that you can land a multi-hero Sonic Wave in fights.

When not much is happening, push out the side lanes.

Pick up {aghanims-shard} after your first few core items as it provides an AoE silence and extra damage. Using it on creep waves also gives you instant wave clear when paired with Scream of Pain.

{aghanims-scepter} is very strong in teamfights. Spam your Shadow Strike on multiple targets to do massive AoE damage once you have it.

## Counter Strategy

Queen of Pain's Shadow Strike is a great harassing spell. Buy extra consumables or dodge it if your hero has a spell that can.

Look to play aggressively on Queen of Pain once her Blink is on cooldown. She is very squishy.

Control power runes against Queen of Pain. She likes to bottle and gank with those.

Be aware of Queen of Pain's {dagon} or {orchid-malevolence} timing. She can solo kill most of the heroes with it.

Avoid fighting in choke spots and clumping up against Queen of Pain's Sonic Wave.

Debuff immunity and magic resistance items are quite good against Queen of Pain.

A well timed defensive Cyclone with {euls-scepter-of-divinity} can practically dodge all of the Sonic Wave burst damage
