## Strategy

The bright yellow Doppelganger illusion takes the same damage as your hero. Bind it to a separate control group to make deceptive plays as the enemies won't be able to tell it apart from your hero while doing damage to it.

Use Spirit Lance to secure ranged creep last hits when you cannot safely walk up with your hero.

Toggle on Phantom Rush on early levels so you don't waste it randomly.

Doppelganger dispels on cast and can be used to disjoint projectile spells and ranged attacks.

Phantom Lancer likes long fights in which you can amass illusions and grind opponents down.

Pick up {aghanims-shard} after your first few core items to have another defensive tool by using Juxtapose to become invisible and escape fights.

## Counter Strategy

Phantom Lancer's main escape mechanism is Doppelganger. As this spell has a very long cooldown at lower levels, look to play aggressively as soon as he uses it

Pressure Phantom Lancer while he's farming {manta-style}. He's very weak until he gets that item. {smoke-of-deceit} on him and place {observer-ward} in his jungle.

Use AoE nukes to weaken Phantom Lancer's illusions to figure out which one is the real hero

Spells like Lion's and Shadow Shaman's Hex and Pugna's Life Drain are able to destroy Phantom Lancer's illusion instantly. That can help when the illusion count is low, but not when there are plenty of them.

Against Phantom Lancer, purchase items that deal AoE damage, preferably magical damage. For example {mjollnir}, {radiance} or {shivas-guard}. {battle-fury} is good as well but Phantom Lancer has a lot of armor and usually evasion on top of that

Phantom Lancer loves long fights as he's able to drain huge amounts of mana. Ideally, look to burst him and to finish the fights reasonably fast. If the fight prolongs, count on the fact that you will be running low on mana and it might be better to disengage.
