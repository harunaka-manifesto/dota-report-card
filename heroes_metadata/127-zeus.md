## Strategy

Lightning Bolt provides vision and reveals invisible heroes and {observer-and-sentry-wards}.

Use Arc Lightning to secure creep last hits and damage the opponents at the same time.

Mana management and rune control are key for Zeus to be able to farm and fight effectively.

Look around the map to kill opponents on low health with Thundergod's Wrath.

Thundergod's Wrath reveals invisible heroes and does damage to them.

Thundergod's Wrath lightens the part of the map where opponents are Smoked and does damage to them.

Don't show yourself at the start of the fight. Let opponents focus on your allies so you can get all of your spells off.

As you are moving around, constantly check for opponents' {observer-ward} with Lightning Bolt.

You can pop {smoke-of-deceit} for yourself here and there and cast spells from it in the fights.

{aghanims-scepter} is a huge power spike against backliners and evasive heroes, such as {tinker}.

{black-king-bar} on enemy heroes is problematic for your damage output. You need to kite those and prolong the fight.

It is fine to use your ultimate here and there in late game to scout for opponents.

Pick up {aghanims-shard} around the 15 minute mark to significantly increase your damage output and farm speed without expending your mana.

## Counter Strategy

Agree on who builds magic resistance items such as {pipe-of-insight} against Zeus - all players should have items with some magic resistance

Place {observer-ward} in unusual spots because of Zeus

Keep your HP above Thundergod's Wrath damage of 300

Control power runes to prevent Zeus from getting arcane or regeneration rune.

{smoke-of-deceit} can be useful to wrap around Zeus to catch him off-guard

{glimmer-cape} is effective against Zeus because it provides passive magic resistance and creates a magic barrier when activated on yourself or allies, helping to reduce his burst damage.
