## Strategy

Spam Eidolons off cooldown to secure last hits and trade with enemy heroes. Avoid feeding them and try to get them to multiply by having each individual Eidolon attack enemy units six times. Also use them to body block the pull camp.

Midnight Pulse destroys trees, which can be useful in certain scenarios to reveal hiding opponents in trees or clear the way for your team.

Once you get {blink-dagger}, make use of it immediately before opponents can see it in your inventory.

Be patient in teamfights - avoid showing too early and wait for opponents to clump up for your combo.

When not much is happening, use Eidolons to push out side lanes safely or farm jungle camps.

## Counter Strategy

Try to kill Eidolons before they multiply.

Be aware of Enigma's {blink-dagger} timing.

Avoid fighting in choke spots and clumping up against {blink-dagger} into Black Hole combo.

Black Hole has a long cooldown. Look for a fight when it is on cooldown.

Stay back with one hero that can cancel Black Hole or save a core that is being Black Holed.

Having Aegis on a core hero to survive through Black Hole is key versus Enigma. Kill Roshan the first chance you get.
