## Strategy

Play in front of your core to make use of your tankiness.

Ignite has a much longer cast range than Fireblast, so cast it first.

Prioritize items that you can Multicast with, such as {scythe-of-vyse}.

Look to Ignite the heroes with {blink-dagger} to keep it canceled continuously.

Look to play in the front and soak up damage alongside your cores if you took the strength talent on level 20.

Bloodlust your right click cores prior and during a fight.

## Counter Strategy

Physical damage doesn't do much against Ogre Magi as he has high starting armor. Either ignore him or try to inflict a lot of damage in a short period of time.

Opt for magic resistance items against Ogre Magi

Ogre Magi players tend to go greedy and farm up {hand-of-midas}. Try to mess with their Midas timing - cooldown is 90 seconds

{lotus-orb} is an effective item against Ogre Magi as both of his damaging spells are targetable

Ogre Magi is scary in late game as he's able to Multicast on his items as well. {black-king-bar} is mandatory for cores and even supports should consider buying it
