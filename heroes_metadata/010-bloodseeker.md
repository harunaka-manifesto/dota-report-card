## Strategy

Use Blood Rite to secure range creep and damage the opponents at the same time if they decide to contest.

Keep an eye on your movement speed. If you are fast, it means someone is very low and you can play more aggressively.

The opponents will often attempt to {town-portal-scroll} out when you Rupture them. Call for an ally to help you out if you don't have a way of securing the kill yourself.

Make sure to Rupture high priority targets in the fights, usually mobile cores, especially with {black-king-bar}.

With {aghanims-shard}, it is often better to stand your ground and fight back when you are low as kills and right clicks will heal you.

## Counter Strategy

Bloodseeker's innate gives him HP sustain by last hitting or denying creeps. Deal bursts of damage to keep him low HP so he cannot last hit creeps with ease and regain HP.

Stay high on HP so that Bloodseeker doesn't gain additional movement speed or see you.

When Ruptured by Bloodseeker, don't move too far and consider {town-portal-scroll} to base if possible.

{lotus-orb}, {linkens-sphere}, {blade-mail} and status resistance items are good at countering Rupture.
