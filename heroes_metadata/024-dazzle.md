PASSIVE: No

## Strategy

Use Poison Touch only if you can follow up with few right clicks on the Poisoned hero. Only your right clicks will refresh its duration.

If an opponent is surrounded by your allied units, you can Shadow Wave those units and deal huge damage.

Use Shadow Wave as Shallow Grave is about to expire to provide a burst of heal.

When in trouble, you can attempt to Shallow Grave yourself and {town-portal-scroll} out.

Avoid showing yourself to opponents at the start of the fight. You are number one priority for them to kill.

Purchase {aghanims-shard} to heal your allies every time you apply a Weave stack on them, practically letting you heal yourself and your allies with all your defensive abilities.

Your Nothl Projection cannot be stunned or silenced. In fights, keep your hero's body in a safe location on a high ground or in tree lines and use the Nothl Projection to save your frontline allies and Poison enemy heroes.

## Counter Strategy

Avoid being hit by Dazzle while under effect of Poison Touch. Move away from him or hide in tree lines before he attacks you.

Shadow Wave does a lot of damage if you are surrounded by enemy units.

Someone should buy a {spirit-vessel} to offset healing from Dazzle and to finish off the Shallow Graved hero.

Look to focus Dazzle in teamfights as he is squishy but provides a lot of sustain if left unchecked.

Buy gap closing items to get on top of Dazzle.

You cannot target Dazzle's Nothl Projection but can still kill him by attacking his real body. Look to find him through the link between his body and the Nothl Projection.
