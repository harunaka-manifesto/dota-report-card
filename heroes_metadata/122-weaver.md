## Rewinds time to reverse damage and position

Weaver has no trouble **evading enemies** to position himself for another strike. Slipping in and out of sight, he avoids death by **rewinding himself to an earlier position and health state**. His arsenal of elusive skills ensures he is always a critical nuisance to his foes.

## Strategy

Consider placing an {observer-ward} for your mid laner quickly with Shukuchi as you load into the game.

Sniping {animal-courier} with the help of a {observer-ward} behind their tier 1 tower will reduce their available detection.

Keep checking opponents' inventory for detection.

Use Shukuchi to secure ranged creep last hits and damage the opponents at the same time.

Weaver is very strong in the early to mid game. Look around the map for opportunities to join the fights.

In fights, focus on applying Swarm on as many opponents as possible and prioritize killing supports and backliners first.

When not much is happening, push out the side lanes aggressively as Weaver is elusive.

Weaver enables the team to take Roshan fairly early due to Swarm armor reduction.

Pick up {aghanims-shard} once you have your damage item in the mid game to further improve your DPS.

{aghanims-scepter} is a game changing pick up. Paired with {blink-dagger} or {aether-lens}, it becomes easier to use.

## Counter Strategy

Bring a {sentry-ward} and extra health consumables to the lane against Weaver.

Remove The Swarm from yourself and from teammates or you will lose a lot of armor.

Weaver's Time Lapse will remove {dust-of-appearance} if it brings him out of the AoE.

Weaver is really good at killing supports and backliners. Protect them.

Using {euls-scepter-of-divinity} on yourself or becoming invisible removes The Swarm, unless you are being detected.

Instant disables and silences are great against the elusive Weaver.

Armor items, {heavens-halberd} and {ghost-scepter} are great against Weaver's physical damage.
